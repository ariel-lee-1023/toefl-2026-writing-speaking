# Technology Impact Daily Well Being

## Prompt (including both student posts)
Dr. Diaz
We’ve been discussing the role of technology in modern society. While some people believe technology has improved our quality of life, others argue that it has created new social and psychological challenges. How do you feel about the impact of technology on our daily lives?

Claire
I think technology has greatly improved our quality of life. It has made communication easier, improved access to information, and created countless opportunities for innovation and growth. Overall, technology has made life more convenient and connected.

Paul
I believe technology has created significant social and psychological challenges. The constant connectivity can lead to stress and burnout, and social media can contribute to feelings of isolation and inadequacy. We need to be mindful of how technology affects our well-being.

## My Polished Response
While I acknowledge that many people who use technology products experience social and psychological pressure, I disagree with Paul's point that such stress and burnout are mainly caused by technology. Technology provides more convenient communication methods and effective tools to help people carry out innovative work, as Claire said, but it does not automatically change our relationships with colleagues or whether we are satisfied with our jobs. What's more, technology gives people access to the knowledge they need to improve relationships and achieve their goals. For example, an online time-management class can teach workers how to organize tasks and reduce stress much more easily than people could in the nineteenth century. Overall, technology enhances our information-seeking skills and makes daily life more efficient.
