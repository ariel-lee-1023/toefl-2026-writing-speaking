## My Key Obstacles Holding You Back from a 5/5
- Running out of time and getting cut off mid-thought due to trailing run-on sentences connected entirely by "because", "and", and "also" (e.g., "...working or waiting for a song").
- Tangled phrasing when attempting to express highly abstract or philosophical ideas, making the argument slightly hard for the rater to follow (e.g., "reflect your ability of the world" and "make music lovers more exciting").

## My What Changed & Why
To elevate the responses to a 5, I applied a Commit-Detail-Elaborate framework to break the long, trailing thoughts into distinct, manageable sentences. I completely preserved the user's highly intellectual original ideas, such as comparing the functional value of music to science and describing the physical excitement of a fast rhythm. However, I replaced the repetitive conjunctions with explicit transition words like "On top of that," "Beyond that," and "For one thing." I also untangled the philosophical phrasing ("reflects our emotional experience of the world") and corrected minor vocabulary slips ("exciting" to "exciting for music lovers"). This addresses the rubric requirement for effective sentence-level connectors, prevents rushing, and ensures the thoughts are completed within the time limit.
