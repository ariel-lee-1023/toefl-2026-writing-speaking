## Q3 Prompt

## Q4 Prompt
Good points. One final question. Some people believe volunteers gain just as much as the people they help, while others believe only those receiving the help truly benefit. Which view do you agree with? Why?

## Q1 My Polished Response
In my local community, the most common volunteer activity is caring for stray animals in urban areas. First, many young people are passionate about animal welfare and feel deeply concerned when they see homeless cats and dogs suffering on the streets. Second, volunteering at local shelters allows residents to provide medical care, food, and temporary shelter, making our city environment safer and more humane for these animals.

## Q2 My Polished Response
If I had one free day each month to help others, I would choose to volunteer as a teacher in a remote, underserved area. First, I am passionate about education and want to share my knowledge with students who lack access to quality learning resources. Second, providing educational support to disadvantaged children equips them with vital skills, which ultimately helps reduce social inequality and creates a better society.

## Q3 My Polished Response
I strongly support the expectation that students and employees should dedicate hours to volunteer work. First, an ideal society cannot rely solely on fierce economic competition; it requires individuals to actively support one another without expecting financial gain. Second, encouraging regular community service fosters empathy and builds stronger social connections, which allows people to collaborate far more harmoniously in their daily lives.

## Q4 My Polished Response
I agree with the view that volunteers gain just as much as the people they serve, provided we accurately measure the impact of their work. First, ensuring that beneficiaries are truly satisfied prevents volunteers from engaging in superficial or self-serving efforts. Second, when volunteers evaluate their performance and see tangible benefits in the community, they gain a profound sense of personal fulfillment and develop essential life skills.
