## Q1 My Polished Response
I usually keep in touch with distant friends and family through daily text messages and weekly video calls. First, texting allows us to share quick updates and photos throughout our busy work schedules without interrupting each other. Second, setting up regular video calls gives us a face-to-face experience where we can have deeper conversations, almost as if we were meeting in person.

## Q2 My Polished Response
A key turning point that changed how I make decisions was receiving a failing grade on an important exam. First, that failure clearly revealed my knowledge gaps and made me realize my old study habits were ineffective. Second, that realization forced me to seek advice from my teacher and completely reorganize my daily study routine, which ultimately made me a far more disciplined student.

## Q3 My Polished Response
I believe that listening well is significantly more important than speaking clearly in effective communication. First, active listening allows you to truly understand the other person's perspective, feelings, and underlying needs. Second, a good listener creates a comfortable atmosphere that encourages open dialogue, which keeps conversations flowing smoothly even during disagreements.

## Q4 My Polished Response
In my view, meeting in person is extremely important for maintaining strong relationships with family and friends. First, face-to-face interactions allow us to read subtle body language and facial expressions, leading to deeper emotional connections and smoother communication. Second, meeting in person eliminates the digital distractions common with phones and computers, allowing us to be fully present with each other.
