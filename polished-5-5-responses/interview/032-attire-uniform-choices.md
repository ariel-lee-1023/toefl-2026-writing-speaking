# Attire Uniform Choices

## Q1 Prompt
Thank you for your participation. I'd like to ask you about your clothing choices. What kinds of clothes do you usually wear in your daily life? Do you tend to dress for comfort or for appearance? Please explain.

## Q2 Prompt
Good. Can you recall a time when you wore something special for an event that was important to you? Please tell me about it and explain why you decided to wear that.

## Q3 Prompt
Interesting. Some schools require their students to wear uniforms every day. Do you agree or disagree that wearing uniforms in school is important for students? Why or why not?

## Q4 Prompt
Good point. For my final question, in your opinion, how important is it for people to choose appropriate clothes when meeting new people for the first time, such as at a job interview, and why?

## Q1 My Polished Response
In my daily life, I typically opt for business casual attire because it strikes an ideal balance between personal comfort and a professional appearance. First, maintaining a streamlined wardrobe of versatile blazers and tailored trousers eliminates decision fatigue during early morning routines, allowing me to prepare for my commute in minutes. Second, this style ensures physical ease throughout long hours at a desk while consistently presenting a polished, credible image to colleagues and clients.

## Q2 My Polished Response
I recall wearing a formal tailored suit for an international moot court competition where I served as an officer. Initially, I felt somewhat constrained because strictly formal attire differs significantly from my casual daily wear, yet adhering to the dress code was essential out of respect for the court. However, once the proceedings began, wearing the suit fostered a strong sense of professional focus, enhancing my confidence and making my presentation feel far more persuasive.

## Q3 My Polished Response
I strongly disagree with requiring students to wear school uniforms daily. First, mandatory dress codes deprive young people of the freedom to select attire that aligns with their personal identity, missing crucial opportunities to develop their aesthetic judgment. Second, allowing students to choose their daily outfits encourages authentic self-expression, which is vital for healthy psychological development during formative academic years.

## Q4 My Polished Response
In my opinion, it is not strictly necessary for individuals to conform to rigid dress standards when meeting someone for the first time. Instead, people should select clothing in which they feel genuinely comfortable, allowing their authentic personality to shine through. First, dressing authentically helps individuals connect more naturally with peers who share similar values. Second, remaining physically comfortable reduces situational anxiety, allowing people to project confidence during high-stakes job interviews or key professional meetings.
