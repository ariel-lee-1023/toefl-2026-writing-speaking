Because everyone is ordering on the internet, the physical stores near your home get less business and are forced to close. As a result, you lose that traditional familiarity with your area, and people become much less connected to their own neighbors.

## Q4 My Polished Response
As I mentioned earlier, it is incredibly important to support small local stores instead of letting big chain stores replace them. For one thing, the managers of these local shops are often your actual neighbors, so buying from them benefits the community and makes you feel good. Beyond that, these small stores cater to specific niches. They can provide highly curated items that massive supermarkets simply do not care to stock.
