# Food Preferences Nutrition

## Q1 Prompt
Thanks for taking part. I'd like to ask you about your food preferences. What kinds of foods do you enjoy eating most? For example, fresh fruits, vegetables, meat or fish, or sweet foods.

## Q2 Prompt
Good. If you had to eat just one of your favourite meals every day for a month, which would you choose and why?

## Q3 Prompt
Interesting. When choosing what to eat, some people believe that flavour is what matters most. Others focus on the impact on their health and bodies. What do you think is more important? Please explain.

## Q4 Prompt
I see. One last question. In your opinion, how important is it for people to know where their food comes from and to learn how it is grown or made? And why?

## Q1 My Polished Response
I enjoy eating a wide variety of food, but I mostly prefer simple, balanced meals like grilled chicken, brown rice, and fresh steamed vegetables. The primary reason is that these wholesome foods provide reliable, clean energy without causing digestive discomfort or afternoon fatigue. For example, having a nutritious lunch with chicken breast and fresh greens gives me a steady stream of nutrients that keeps me focused throughout my work day. In addition, basic whole foods are easily accessible and quick to prepare, making them both healthy and practical for a busy schedule. Therefore, prioritizing simple, nutrient-dense meals remains my daily preference.

## Q2 My Polished Response
If I had to eat just one meal every day for a full month, I would definitely choose Hainanese chicken rice. The main reason is that it offers a comforting, well-balanced flavor profile that never feels overwhelming or heavy on the stomach. For instance, when I suffered from a severe cold last winter, warm chicken rice was the only dish I could easily digest because the poached chicken and fragrant broth were so soothing. Furthermore, this dish provides an ideal mix of lean protein, carbohydrates, and healthy fats, ensuring that my daily nutritional requirements are met without sacrificing taste. Consequently, its combination of gentle comfort and balanced nutrition makes it the perfect choice for a long-term meal.

## Q3 My Polished Response
When choosing what to eat, I firmly believe that prioritizing physical health is far more important than focusing solely on temporary flavor. The primary reason is that good physical health serves as the essential foundation for a vibrant, productive life. For instance, if a person consistently consumes high-fat, high-sugar meals merely for immediate enjoyment, they will eventually suffer from low energy, weight gain, and chronic health issues that disrupt their long-term goals. While I certainly enjoy a delicious treat on occasion, making health-conscious choices ensures sustained vitality and prevents future medical complications. Thus, making long-term bodily health my top priority is far more rewarding than chasing fleeting taste preferences.

## Q4 My Polished Response
In my opinion, it is extremely important for people to understand where their food comes from and how it is produced. The biggest reason is that knowing the origins of our food empowers us to make healthier, more responsible dietary choices instead of blindly relying on heavily processed convenience items. For example, learning how local farms harvest seasonal produce encourages consumers to choose whole foods rather than mass-produced snacks loaded with artificial ingredients. Additionally, understanding food production fosters awareness of environmental sustainability and ethical farming practices. Ultimately, being informed about food sourcing protects our personal health and supports a more responsible, sustainable food system.
