## Q1 Prompt
Thank you for taking the time to speak with me. I'd like to ask you about your sports preferences. What kinds of sports do you most enjoy playing? For example, do you usually play soccer, basketball, tennis, or something else?

## Q2 Prompt
Great. When you watch sports, do you prefer to watch at home, or to attend the games in person? Why?

## Q3 Prompt
Interesting. Some people say that playing sports teaches young people important lessons about working together and effort, while others say it is mainly just for fun with little real value. What do you think, and why?

## Q4 Prompt
Good points. I just have one more question. Do you agree or disagree that supporting a local sports team helps bring people in a community closer together? Why or why not?

## Q1 My Polished Response
My favorite sport to play is soccer because it offers an ideal balance between intense cardiovascular exercise and strategic teamwork. First, the fast-paced nature of the game forces players to maintain continuous physical movement while constantly reading the field, which builds athletic endurance and sharpens tactical decision-making skills. Second, it is exceptionally accessible to play anywhere since it requires minimal equipment—just a single ball and an open field—making it easy to organize quick matches with friends.

## Q2 My Polished Response
While I appreciate the energetic atmosphere of stadium games, I still prefer watching sports at home because crowded venues often feel overwhelming. First, staying at home allows me to completely control my environment while enjoying a clear, unobstructed view of the action. Second, modern high-definition broadcasts provide multiple camera angles and instant replays, ensuring I can follow every critical play in complete comfort without dealing with long stadium lines.

## Q3 My Polished Response
I strongly believe that participating in youth sports teaches valuable life lessons rather than serving as mere entertainment. First, organized sports teach children how to handle setbacks with resilience, helping them view a loss as an opportunity for personal growth rather than a discouraging failure. Second, competing on a team instills essential skills in communication and collaboration that prepare young people to work effectively alongside others throughout their lives.

## Q4 My Polished Response
I firmly agree that supporting a local sports team helps bring community members closer together. First, when residents gather to cheer for the same franchise, that shared enthusiasm fosters positive interpersonal relationships and a strong sense of civic identity. Second, celebrating memorable victories together at local venues creates a supportive social network across diverse groups, bridging generational and background divides throughout the neighborhood.
