# Environmental Habits Responsibilities

## Q1 Prompt
Thank you for taking the time to speak with me. I'd like to ask you about your environmental habits. What do you usually do at home to help the environment?

## Q2 Prompt
Very good. In your opinion, what is the most important environmental problem that people around the world are facing today? Please explain.

## Q3 Prompt
Interesting. Some people believe individuals are mainly responsible for protecting the environment, while others believe governments and companies should take the lead. Which view comes closer to your own and why?

## Q4 Prompt
Good points. I just have one more question. In your opinion, how important is it for people to support businesses that try not to harm the environment, even if their products cost more? Please explain.

## Q1 My Polished Response
At home, my primary habit for protecting the environment is avoiding single-use plastic bottles. Instead of purchasing bottled water, I always use a reusable glass container to drink water throughout the day. First, this simple personal habit significantly reduces my daily plastic waste consumption. Second, using durable glassware encourages my family members to adopt similar eco-friendly practices in our household.

## Q2 My Polished Response
In my opinion, global warming is the most urgent environmental problem facing the world today. First, rising global temperatures cause increasingly unpredictable and extreme weather patterns, such as intense heatwaves in the summer and severe rainstorms. Second, these extreme temperatures force people to run air conditioning units continuously, which consumes massive amounts of electricity and further escalates the climate crisis.

## Q3 My Polished Response
I believe that environmental protection is primarily the responsibility of individual citizens rather than just governments or corporations. First, while governments can enact environmental laws and policies, these regulations are only effective if individual citizens actively choose to follow them in daily life. Second, companies only develop and supply green technologies if there is strong consumer demand in the market driving that change.

## Q4 My Polished Response
I do not think it is necessary for consumers to support expensive eco-friendly businesses if cheaper, sustainable options exist. First, many products marketed as environmentally friendly carry an unjustifiably high price markup without proven environmental benefits. Second, much of this green technology is still in its early stages of development, meaning consumers can achieve similar ecological results by adopting simple, low-cost sustainable habits instead.
