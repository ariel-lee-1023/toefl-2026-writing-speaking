# Clothing Choices Dress Codes

## Q1 Prompt
Thank you for your participation. I'd like to ask you about your clothing choices. What kinds of clothes do you usually wear in your daily life? Do you tend to dress for comfort or for appearance? Please explain.

## Q2 Prompt
Good. Can you recall a time when you wore something special for an event that was important to you? Please tell me about it and explain why you decided to wear that.

## Q3 Prompt
Interesting. Some schools require their students to wear uniforms every day. Do you agree or disagree that wearing uniforms in school is important for students? Why or why not?

## Q4 Prompt
Good point. For my final question, in your opinion, how important is it for people to choose appropriate clothes when meeting new people for the first time, such as at a job interview, and why?

## Q1 My Polished Response
In my daily life, I usually choose business casual clothing because it balances comfort and professional appearance. First, keeping a streamlined business casual closet makes dressing in the morning fast and convenient without wasting time selecting outfits. Second, these clothes allow me to feel relaxed throughout the day while still maintaining a tidy, respectable appearance for work.

## Q2 My Polished Response
I cannot recall a specific event that required me to wear unusually formal attire. First, my daily routine and professional environment generally allow for a relaxed, functional dress code. Second, I intentionally select versatile, classic outfits for my standard wardrobe, so I rarely need to purchase or wear specialized clothing for unique events.

## Q3 My Polished Response
I strongly disagree with the idea that students should be required to wear school uniforms. First, choosing their own clothes allows students to exercise personal autonomy and explore their unique sense of aesthetics. Second, allowing personal dress choices ensures that students can prioritize their own physical comfort, which creates a more pleasant and effective learning environment.

## Q4 My Polished Response
In my opinion, choosing appropriate attire when meeting new people, such as in a job interview, is extremely important. First, how you dress reflects your personal habits and shows respect for the organization's professional standards. Second, dressing appropriately for the workplace helps you feel more confident and reduces unnecessary stress during crucial first impressions.
