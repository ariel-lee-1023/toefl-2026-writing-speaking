# Hobbies Free Time

## Q1 Prompt
Thank you for participating. Today, I'd like to ask you about your hobbies and free time activities. What kind of activities do you enjoy doing in your free time?

## Q2 Prompt
Great! Do you prefer spending your free time alone or with others? Why?

## Q3 Prompt
Interesting. Some people believe that having hobbies can significantly improve one's mental health. Do you agree or disagree? Why?

## Q4 Prompt
Good points. I just have one more question. In the future, do you think people will have more or less free time for hobbies and leisure activities? What factors do you think will influence this?

## Q1 My Polished Response
Playing soccer in my free time is definitely my favorite activity. For one thing, running on the pitch provides an incredible physical workout that keeps me energized. Beyond the fitness aspect, competing in team matches helps me sharpen my strategic thinking and build strong camaraderie with my teammates. Just last weekend, our team organized a friendly match, and working together to score in the final minutes made me feel truly fulfilled and connected to the group.

## Q2 My Polished Response
I definitely prefer spending my free time with others because soccer is fundamentally a collective sport. Engaging in group activities gives me a natural opportunity to bond with close friends and expand my social circle. For instance, after our matches, my teammates and I usually grab lunch together to discuss our favorite professional clubs, review our performance on the field, and even share study advice. These shared experiences make leisure time far more meaningful than practicing alone.

## Q3 My Polished Response
I completely agree that pursuing hobbies significantly improves mental well-being. Engaging in personal passions provides a necessary mental break from daily stress and academic pressure. When people immerse themselves in activities they genuinely love—especially outdoor sports—it lowers their stress levels and allows them to reconnect with nature. For example, spending two hours playing sports outdoors helps clear my mind, giving me a fresh perspective when I return to my daily routine.

## Q4 My Polished Response
I believe people will have significantly more free time for hobbies in the future, largely due to rapid advancements in artificial intelligence. As AI and automation continue to streamline labor-intensive and routine daily tasks, the overall demand for manual working hours will decrease. As a result, individuals will no longer need to spend forty hours a week at traditional jobs, allowing them to dedicate more time to creative interests, personal wellness, and family activities.
