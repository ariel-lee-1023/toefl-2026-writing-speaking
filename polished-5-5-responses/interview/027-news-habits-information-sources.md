# News Habits Information Sources

## Q1 Prompt
Thank you for taking the time to speak with me. I'd like to ask you about news and information. Are there any kinds of news you follow more closely than others? Like sports, weather, world events, or something else?

## Q2 Prompt
I see. When you check the news, do you prefer to do it in the morning or later in the evening? Why?

## Q3 Prompt
Interesting. These days, many people get their news from social media instead of newspapers or television. Do you think people are better informed when they get news this way? Why or why not?

## Q4 Prompt
Good points. I just have one more question. Do you agree or disagree that following news from other countries is just as important as following local news? Why or why not?

## Q1 My Polished Response
I follow technological news far more closely than any other category. The primary reason is that staying updated on emerging industry trends directly enables me to streamline my daily workflow and optimize my productivity. Furthermore, as an engineering student preparing to enter the job market, tracking tech developments helps me identify high-growth fields and cultivate practical skills required for my future career. For instance, following recent updates in AI tools led me to learn Python last semester, which immediately opened up new internship opportunities.

## Q2 My Polished Response
I strongly prefer to check the news in the evening after my daily work is finished. First, delaying news consumption until the end of the day keeps my mornings free from distraction, allowing me to focus entirely on my primary tasks without feeling anxious or overwhelmed by breaking headlines. Second, reviewing the day's events in the evening gives me dedicated time to process and reflect on what happened. For example, reading evening analytical pieces helps me digest complex stories calmly before going to sleep.

## Q3 My Polished Response
In my opinion, people are generally less informed when they rely primarily on social media for their news. First, social media platforms are algorithmically designed to maximize user engagement rather than deliver accurate reporting. Sensation-driven headlines and outrage-inducing posts are prioritized over verified facts simply because they generate more clicks. Second, continuous exposure to fragmented stories prevents readers from gaining a comprehensive understanding of complex topics. Relying on short posts makes it difficult to distinguish objective analysis from biased opinion.

## Q4 My Polished Response
I disagree with the idea that international news is just as important as local news, as I believe local reporting deserves greater priority. First, local events directly impact our daily lives, safety, and community infrastructure. Information about municipal policies leads to immediate practical action, such as attending town hall meetings to address local school budgets. Second, strong civic engagement at home allows citizens to solve immediate problems within their own neighborhoods before attempting to understand global issues. Therefore, staying grounded in local news must come first.
