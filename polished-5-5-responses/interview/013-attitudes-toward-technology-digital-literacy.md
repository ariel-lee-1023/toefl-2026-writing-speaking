## Q4 My Polished Response
In my view, new technology generally makes life better, even though it can sometimes amplify underlying human problems. On the positive side, modern digital tools allow us to access information rapidly and solve complex daily tasks, which significantly enhances overall convenience. However, technology cannot automatically resolve pre-existing personal issues—such as weak communication in relationships—and it often makes these underlying friction points more obvious. Overall, as long as we use these tools mindfully, technology serves as a net positive for society.
