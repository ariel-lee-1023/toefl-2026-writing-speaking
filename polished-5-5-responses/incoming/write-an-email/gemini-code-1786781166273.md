## Title
Rooftop Community Garden

## Prompt
You recently moved into a new apartment building. Your neighbor, Mr. Okonkwo, has organized a community garden on the building's rooftop for several years. You would like to join the garden project and grow some vegetables this summer.
Write an email to Mr. Okonkwo. In your email, do the following:

* Introduce yourself and explain your interest in gardening.

* Describe what you would like to grow.

* Ask how to join the project.

Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

## My Polished Response
Dear Mr. Okonkwo,

My name is Alex, and I moved into apartment 4B just a few weeks ago. I have always enjoyed gardening and was genuinely pleased to discover that you have organized the rooftop community garden for several years.

This summer I would especially like to grow tomatoes, zucchini, and a couple of culinary herbs such as basil and rosemary. These plants do well in sunny rooftop conditions and would let me cook with ingredients I have raised myself.

Could you please let me know how a new resident can join the project? I would appreciate details on whether any plots are still available, how the maintenance and watering responsibilities are shared, and whether you hold a short orientation for newcomers. I am free most weekday evenings if a brief meeting would be helpful.

Thank you for creating this valuable community space. I look forward to hearing from you.

Best regards,  
Alex  
Apartment 4B

## My Diagnosis
The draft is a solid Score 4: all three required actions are present, the tone is polite, and the request for practical details shows clear organization. The ceiling is created by language-control issues that keep the response from “almost no lexical or grammatical errors” and from fully consistent precision. Exact problem spots include the missing space and period in “Dear Mr.Okonkwo.”, the spelling “absolutly”, the run-on “I am Alex,moved to this apartment recently,and impressed by our community”, the subject-verb mismatch “This rooftop is so fascinating and match my interest”, the missing article and awkward structure in “I would like to suggest that grow summer vegetables”, the singular “resident” instead of “residents”, and the fragment plus misspelling “Looking forward to your expliation.” These do not block communication, which is why the response remains generally successful, but they prevent the near-error-free standard of a 5.

## My What Changed & Why
The polished version keeps the same three-part structure and the same core ideas while fixing every surface error and adding the concrete details the Score 5 band rewards. The introduction is rewritten as two connected sentences that cleanly present identity, recent arrival, and genuine interest, eliminating the run-on and the missing article. The plant description now names tomatoes, zucchini, basil, and rosemary and supplies a realistic purpose (home cooking), turning the generic phrase into effective elaboration. The request is kept in polite interrogative form and expanded with an offer of availability, which simultaneously strengthens social conventions and shows readiness to participate. The closing is reduced to one complete expression of thanks plus name and apartment number, removing the redundant second thanks and the misspelled fragment. The result meets all four Score 5 criteria: elaboration that fully supports the communicative purpose, effective syntactic variety with precise word choice, consistent appropriate social conventions, and almost no lexical or grammatical errors beyond those expected under timed conditions.
