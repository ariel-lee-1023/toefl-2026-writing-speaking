## Title
Photography Exhibition at Sunrise Coffee

## Prompt
You are the vice president of your university's photography club. The club would like to organize a photo exhibition at a local café, Sunrise Coffee, to display members' work. You have heard that the café has hosted similar events before and want to inquire about the possibility.
Write an email to the café owner, Ms. Reyes. In your email, do the following:

* Explain who you are and what the club would like to do.

* Describe the type of exhibition you have in mind.

* Ask about availability and any requirements.

Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

## My Polished Response
Dear Ms. Reyes,

I am the vice president of the university’s student photography club. I am writing to inquire about the possibility of hosting a photography exhibition at your café next month to showcase our members’ recent work.

We envision a two-week exhibition centered on the theme of urban rivers, featuring approximately fifteen to twenty framed, medium-format prints that capture the interplay between local waterways, city bridges, and riverbank life.

Could you please let us know whether you have wall space available for a two-week display during late September or early October? We would also appreciate any details about your requirements for hanging artwork and guidelines for preventing wall damage.

Thank you very much for your time and for your continued support of local young artists. I look forward to hearing from you.

Sincerely,  
Alex
