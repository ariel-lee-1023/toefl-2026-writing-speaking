## Title
Green Mountain Inn Inquiry

## Prompt
You are a member of your university’s hiking club and have volunteered to arrange hotel accommodations for the club’s upcoming trip to Green Mountain. There are fifteen to twenty people in your group, and you will need to check out at 4 a.m. to start hiking. Another member has recommended Green Mountain Inn, where he has stayed before.
Write an email to Lisa Ngo, manager at Green Mountain Inn. In your email, do the following.
Explain how you heard about the inn.
Describe your schedule and needs.
Ask for availability and room rates.
Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

## My Polished Response
Dear Ms. Ngo, I am writing on behalf of our university hiking club to inquire about lodging for an upcoming weekend trip to Green Mountain. A member who previously stayed at your establishment recommended it highly, praising both the facilities and the hospitality. Our group will consist of fifteen to twenty people, and we are hoping to reserve a block of rooms for the night before our hike. Because we plan to hit the trail before sunrise, we will need to check out at 4:00 a.m., so I would like to confirm that your front desk can accommodate such an early departure. Could you please let me know your availability for a group of this size, along with your current room rates and any applicable group discounts? Sincerely, Alex Reed.
