## Title
Rooftop Community Garden Membership

## Prompt
You recently moved into a new apartment building. Your neighbor, Mr. Okonkwo, has organized a community garden on the building's rooftop for several years. You would like to join the garden project and grow some vegetables this summer. Write an email to Mr. Okonkwo.
In your email, do the following:

Introduce yourself and explain your interest in gardening. Describe what you would like to grow. Ask how to join the project. Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

## My Polished Response
Dear Mr. Okonkwo,

My name is Ariel Lee, and I recently moved into apartment 4B. I'm writing to ask about joining the rooftop garden this summer. I find caring for plants a relaxing way to unwind after work, and I'd welcome the chance to get to know other residents through a shared activity.

I'd love to grow tomatoes and carrots, since I use both regularly in salads and soups. Although I have limited experience growing vegetables, I'm eager to learn from the more experienced gardeners and would appreciate your advice on varieties suited to the rooftop.

Could you let me know whether any plots are available and what I need to do to sign up? I'd also appreciate information about any membership fees and shared maintenance duties. I'm usually free on weekday evenings, so I could help with watering then.

Thank you for coordinating the garden. I look forward to hearing from you.

Best regards,
Ariel Lee
