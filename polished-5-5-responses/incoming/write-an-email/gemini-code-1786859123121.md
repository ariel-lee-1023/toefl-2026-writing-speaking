## Title
Requesting Missed Notes

## Prompt
Absent from Class / Request Notes
You were absent from an important class and need to ask a classmate, Maria, for her notes and any key information you missed. You also want to thank her in advance for her help.
Write an email to Maria. In your email, do the following:
* Explain why you did not attend the lecture.
* Request her help and ask whether she has time to help you catch up.
* Thank her in advance for her assistance.

## My Polished Response
Hi Maria,
I hope you are doing well. I missed yesterday's economics lecture because I developed a high fever and had to visit the campus clinic. Since the professor introduced the final research project and explained several new concepts, I am worried that the slides alone will not be enough for me to catch up.
Could you please share your notes with me? If you have time, I would also appreciate meeting for twenty minutes after class tomorrow so I can ask about the main examples and the project requirements. I will read the textbook section beforehand and prepare a short list of questions, so I will not take too much of your time.
Thank you very much for helping me. I would be happy to proofread your project outline or buy you coffee in return. Please let me know what time works best.
Best,
Alex Lee
