## Title
Roman Law Course Inquiry

## Prompt
You are a student starting a new semester, and you would like some additional information about a course you are interested in that is taught by Dr. Adams. You want to ensure that the course is suitable for you.

Write an email to Dr. Adams. In your email, do the following:

- Explain why you are interested in the course.
- Ask for the additional information you would like to have about the course.
- Thank Dr. Adams for taking the time to respond.

Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

Personalized scenario: The course is Roman Law. Ask for its syllabus and clarify whether it primarily studies selected Roman legal texts and the rules within particular areas of law or also examines the historical evolution of the Roman legal system and its substantive content. Express a stronger interest in the historical approach.

## My Polished Response
Dear Dr. Adams,

I am considering taking your Roman Law course this semester because I am interested in how legal systems develop over time.

Could you please share the course syllabus and clarify its main focus? Does the course primarily examine selected Roman legal texts to explain rules in areas such as property and obligations, or does it also trace changes in the structure and content of Roman law across different historical periods?

I am particularly interested in the latter approach, especially how legal institutions and rules evolved in response to changing social conditions. Knowing how much emphasis the course places on this historical perspective would help me decide whether it matches my interests.

Thank you for taking the time to answer my questions.

Sincerely,
Ariel
