## Title
Thinking Fast Slow Book Meeting

## Prompt
You are a member of the campus book club and are hosting the next meeting. You have selected a book and want to inform the club's members about your choice and about other meeting details.

Write an email to the members of the book club. In your email, do the following.

- Describe the book you have chosen and why you selected it.
- Explain how this book fits with the club's interests.
- Provide details about the date, time, and location of the next meeting.

Write as much as you can and in complete sentences. Use your own words.

To: Book Club Members
Subject: Book selection and meeting details

## My Polished Response
Dear Book Club Members,

For our next meeting, I have chosen Thinking, Fast and Slow by Daniel Kahneman. The book explores how quick judgments and more deliberate thinking shape our decisions. I selected it because it encourages us to question assumptions we often take for granted.

Since our club enjoys connecting nonfiction to everyday life, this book should give us plenty to discuss. For example, we could share situations in which a first impression influenced a choice and consider whether thinking more carefully would have changed it.

We will meet on Thursday, October 15, from 6:30 to 9:00 p.m. in Seminar Room 204 of the Student Center. Please bring one passage you found thought-provoking and a question for the group.

I look forward to hearing your thoughts!

Best,
Ariel
