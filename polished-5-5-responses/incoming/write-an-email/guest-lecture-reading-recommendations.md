## Title
Guest Lecture Follow-Up

## Prompt
Background:
You recently attended a guest lecture at your university on environmental sustainability. You found the lecture highly informative and would like to ask the speaker, Dr. Roberts, for recommendations on further reading materials and resources. You also want to know if she offers lectures on other topics that interest you.

Requirements:
1. Mention what you found informative about her lecture
2. Request recommendations for further reading materials and resources on environmental sustainability
3. Ask her if she gives lectures on other topics that interest you

## My Polished Response
Dear Dr. Roberts,

Thank you for your recent guest lecture on environmental sustainability. Your examples of campus waste reduction helped me understand how everyday decisions can contribute to broader environmental goals.

I'd like to explore the subject further. Could you recommend an introductory book and a few reliable articles or online resources suitable for an undergraduate student? I'm particularly interested in how universities can put sustainability principles into practice.

I'm also interested in urban climate adaptation and sustainable transportation. Do you give lectures on either of these topics? If so, I would appreciate any information about upcoming sessions or recordings I could watch.

Thank you for your time and for making the subject so accessible.

Sincerely,
Ariel
