## Title
Gym Equipment Problems

## Prompt
Gym Equipment Problems
You are a member of a gym and have noticed that some of the exercise equipment is problematic.
Write an email to Ms. Taylor. In your email, do the following:
* Describe the issues you have noticed with the current equipment.
* Explain why adding new equipment would improve the gym experience.
* Explain the possible consequences for membership if the services are not improved.

## My Polished Response
Dear Ms. Taylor,
I am writing to report several problems with the exercise equipment at the gym. Two treadmills stop suddenly even when users are moving at a moderate speed, and one weight machine has a loose cable. These problems make workouts inconvenient and could cause serious injuries.
Adding or replacing equipment would improve the gym experience in two ways. First, members would spend less time waiting during busy evening hours. Second, reliable machines would allow people to follow their training plans safely and consistently. I suggest arranging an immediate safety inspection, repairing the loose cable, and replacing the two oldest treadmills.
If the equipment is not improved, members may cancel their memberships and choose another gym. I enjoy the friendly atmosphere here, so I hope the problem can be addressed soon.
Thank you for your attention.
Best,
Alex Lee
