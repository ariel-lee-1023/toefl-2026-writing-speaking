## Title
Emma Surprise Party Help

## Prompt
You are planning a surprise birthday party for your friend Emma. You need to buy decorations, order a cake, and invite guests, among other things. You are busy with school and would like your friend, John, to help you.
Write an email to John. In your email, do the following.

* Explain why you need help.

* Describe the tasks that you need help with.

* Suggest a date and time to meet and discuss the party plans.

Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

## My Polished Response
To: John  
Subject: Help with Emma’s surprise party  

Dear John,  

I’m organizing a surprise birthday party for Emma next month and could really use your help. My course load has been heavier than usual this semester, so I don’t have enough free time to handle everything myself.  

Specifically, I need someone to pick up the decorations and balloons from the party store downtown, place the cake order at the bakery on Main Street, and help me finalize the guest list and send the invitations. You already know most of the people we’re inviting, which would make the process much faster.  

Would you be free to meet this Friday at 4:30 p.m. in the student center café so we can go over the details together? Let me know if that time works or if another afternoon is better for you.  

Thanks so much,  
Alex

Removed the confusing Google Drive reference and the incomplete final sentence. Cleaned all spelling, agreement, and collocation errors so language use no longer interferes with readability.
