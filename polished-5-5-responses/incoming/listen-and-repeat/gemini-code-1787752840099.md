## Title
Coffee Machine Operation

## Prompt
Scenario: A supervisor training you on operating a coffee machine at a cafe.

1. First, fill the water tank with fresh water.
2. Place a coffee filter in the slot.
3. Use the timer to brew before you wake up each morning.
4. Put your cup on the drip tray before percolation begins.
5. Select your preferred beverage strength on the control panel.
6. You can avert disastrous spills and messes by not overfilling the water tank.
7. For the best possible performance, you will need to clean your coffee machine at least once per month.

## My Chunking & Memory Strategy
Short imperatives establish immediate action-object units without introductory filler. Medium sentences rely on temporal cues such as before to mark transitions between the main action and the time chunk. Long sentences use conditional openings like for the best possible performance to set up the obligation clause before delivering the core action.

## My Pronunciation Focus
- Compressed function words: the in sentence 3, will in sentence 7
- Word endings (-s / -ed / final t-d): begins, spills, messes, and crisp final t in avert
- Rhythm & stress: stress content verbs avert and Select; avoid substituting /ʃ/ for /s/ in messes
- Content words to say crisply: cup, avert, messes, filter, timer
