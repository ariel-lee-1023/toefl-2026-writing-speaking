## Title
Self Service Ticket Kiosk

## Prompt
Scenario: A supervisor training an usher on how to assist guests using a theater's ticket kiosk.

1. Welcome guests to our self-service ticket kiosk.
2. Tap the start button on the touch screen.
3. Choose the number of tickets for the whole group.
4. Tap any open seat to add it to the order.
5. Insert a credit card into the slot for payment.
6. Guests who arrive late may sit only in the open back rows of the theater.
7. The kiosk will not accept cash payments, so use a credit card instead.

## My Chunking & Memory Strategy
Boundaries align naturally with syntactic switches (action → place → purpose). For long sentences like #6 and #7, identifying key structural anchors—such as the relative pronoun "who" in #6 or the logical connector "so" in #7—creates clear chunk breaks that prevent memory overload and lexical substitution.
