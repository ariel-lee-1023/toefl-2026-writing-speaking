## Title
Paint Department Service Guide

## Prompt
Scenario: A supervisor guiding a store associate through custom paint mixing procedures.

1. Show the customer our wall of color samples.
2. Scan the chosen color chip into the system.
3. Pick the right base can for the chosen color.
4. Tighten the lid clamp before starting the mixer.
5. Run the mixer twice for darker shades or metallic finishes.
6. Recommend a wide brush that works best for the customer's chosen paint finish.
7. Before the customer leaves, give them the matching sample card for future touch-ups.

## My Chunking & Memory Strategy
Boundaries follow natural category transitions (action → place/purpose/time). In sentence #6, the relative pronoun "that" acts as a structural anchor to isolate the description chunk ("that works best"), preventing missing articles or inserted nouns. In sentence #7, the introductory time clause ("Before the customer leaves") creates a clear front-loaded boundary that protects the downstream command.

## My Pronunciation Focus
- Compressed function words: "of" in "wall of"; "into the" in location chunks; "for the" / "that" in purpose and description chunks.
- Word endings (-s / -ed / final t-d): samples, shades, finishes, works, leaves, touch-ups.
- Rhythm & stress: Maintain stress on core content words ("color samples", "color chip", "base can", "lid clamp", "metallic finishes", "sample card").
- Content words to say crisply: samples (/ˈsæm.pəlz/), lid (/lɪd/), shades (/ʃeɪdz/), sample card (/ˈsæm.pəl kɑːrd/).
