## Title
University Research Lab

## Prompt
Scenario: You are being trained to help students navigate the university research lab. Listen to your trainer and repeat what your trainer says. Repeat only once.

1. Please sign in over here at the entrance.
2. Find the workstation assigned to you.
3. Return supplies to the correct shelf so others can find them.
4. The safety equipment is located here behind the desks.
5. The fume hood should always be used for hazardous materials.
6. To prevent accidents from occurring, we insist on proper waste disposal procedures.
7. Before beginning your lab work, you should review the posted safety instructions.

## My Chunking & Memory Strategy
Item 1 keeps sign in as an action expression; in is not the start of the entrance phrase. Item 2 holds "assigned to you" as a reduced passive description of the workstation, without inserting that is. Item 3 contains an action, a destination, and a so-clause explaining the result/purpose. Item 4 retains equipment as a singular mass noun and desks as plural. Item 5 keeps "should always be used" as one modal passive predicate. Item 6 opens with purpose and includes the pattern "prevent accidents from occurring"; the main clause then uses the separate expression "insist on". Item 7 opens with Before plus an -ing phrase, followed by modal advice and its object. The front-loaded cues in the last two items identify boundaries without predicting or replacing the exact words. Word counts treat contractions and hyphenated compounds as one word. The length labels are approximate and do not determine an official task position.

## My Pronunciation Focus
- Compressed function words: Sign in needs its particle in. Destination takes to in "to the correct shelf", while location takes behind in "behind the desks". For introduces the fume hood's purpose. The prevention expression needs from before occurring, and insist needs on before the procedures. In the last item, preserve "Before beginning your lab work" exactly, including your after beginning.
- Word endings (-s / -ed / final t-d): Assigned, located, used, and posted retain their endings. Supplies, others, desks, materials, accidents, procedures, and instructions remain plural. Find and hood end in d; correct, equipment, prevent, insist, and waste end in t. Equipment does not gain a plural -s; workstation, shelf, disposal, work, and review also stay in their supplied forms. Hazardous ends in a lexical s sound rather than a plural ending. Endings cannot be recovered from chunk shape alone.
- Rhythm & stress: No audio was supplied, so the speaker's stress pattern and any rhythm substitution cannot be identified. The following are text-based phrasing suggestions only: Keep the passive predicates complete, especially "should always be used". Treat the opening purpose in item 6 and time phrase in item 7 as coherent units before their main clauses.
- Content words to say crisply: entrance, workstation, assigned, supplies, equipment, fume, hazardous, occurring, disposal, procedures, posted, instructions.
