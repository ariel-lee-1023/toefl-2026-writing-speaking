## Title
Music Preferences Habits

## Q1 Prompt
Thank you for your participation. I'd like to learn about your music habits. What kinds of music do you most enjoy listening to in your free time? For example, do you usually prefer pop, rock, classical, or other styles?

## Q2 Prompt
Great. Can you recall a time when a particular song or piece of music affected your mood or feelings? Please explain.

## Q3 Prompt
Interesting. Some people think that schools should teach music as a required subject. Others believe schools should focus only on subjects like math and science instead. What is your opinion? Please explain.

## Q4 Prompt
Good points. I just have one more question. In the future, do you think people will mostly listen to music alone at home? Or will live concerts and events still be popular? Why?

## Q1 My Polished Response
Honestly, the music genre I love listening to the most is pop rap. The biggest reason is that it features an intense rhythm and really enjoyable melodies. For me, this keeps me energetic and helps me work much more efficiently. On top of that, the lyrics are often fascinating and have a unique aesthetic that I really appreciate.

## Q2 My Polished Response
A great example is a pop-rock song called Goodies. I love this particular track because the rhythm literally makes my heart jump fast, which gives me an immediate sense of excitement. Beyond that, the song provides me with a huge burst of energy. Because of this, it is really enjoyable to listen to whether I am studying, working, or just waiting around.

## Q3 My Polished Response
I strongly believe that music is just as important as science. While science helps us understand facts, music reflects our emotional experience of the world and teaches us how to positively influence the feelings of others. To me, that is a truly authentic human ability. Cultivating this appreciation makes people love the world more, meaning music serves an equally essential function as any science subject.

## Q4 My Polished Response
I definitely think live concerts will become even more popular in the future. For one thing, people who attend are not just there to enjoy the songs; they actively want to meet other people who share their exact same preferences. Getting to form a real connection with both the crowd and the singers makes the whole experience so much more exciting for music lovers than just listening alone at home.
