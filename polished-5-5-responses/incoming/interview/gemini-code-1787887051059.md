## Title
Gift Giving Habits and Experiences

## Q1 Prompt
Thank you for your participation. I'd like to ask you about your gift-giving habits. What kinds of gifts do you usually give to family members or friends? For example, food, clothes, books, or flowers?

## Q2 Prompt
I see. When you give a gift to someone, do you prefer to choose something practical and useful or something fun and surprising? Why?

## Q3 Prompt
Interesting, in recent years more people have started giving experiences like concert tickets or restaurant meals as gifts instead of physical things. What do you think are the main reasons for this trend?

## Q4 Prompt
Good points. I just have one last question. Do you agree or disagree that the thought behind a gift matters more than how expensive it is? Why or why not?

## Q1 My Polished Response
When selecting gifts for friends or family, I generally prefer giving carefully chosen books rather than tangible material items. First, a well-written book offers enduring intellectual value rather than momentary entertainment. Second, sharing a meaningful book creates opportunities for ongoing personal connection. For instance, when I gave my sister a history book about World War I, it sparked a series of engaging conversations that brought us much closer together.

## Q2 My Polished Response
When selecting a gift for someone who is not a close friend, I prefer choosing something fun and interactive, such as a strategy board game or card game. First, selecting an entertaining game creates immediate social engagement without feeling overly personal or intimate. Second, an interactive game serves as an excellent icebreaker in group settings, quickly getting people involved and sparking lively conversation throughout the gathering.

## Q3 My Polished Response
I believe people increasingly prefer giving special experiences like concert tickets primarily because modern urban lifestyles have become far more space-constrained. First, many city residents simply lack the physical storage for additional material objects, making clutter-free experiences much more practical. Second, physical items often yield only temporary satisfaction before losing their novelty, whereas shared experiences create lasting memories and strengthen personal relationships over time.

## Q4 My Polished Response
I firmly agree that the intention and thought behind a gift matter far more than its monetary price tag. First, genuine relationships are sustained through mutual understanding and emotional connection rather than superficial displays of financial status. Second, taking the time to select a personalized gift tailored to a recipient's specific passions demonstrates true care, whereas simply purchasing an expensive item can often feel transactional and impersonal.
