## Title
Travel Essentials and Future Travel Trends

## Q1 Prompt
Thank you for joining the study. I'd like to ask you about your travel experiences. What items are essential for you when you travel? Do you tend to pack light or take as much as possible? Please explain.

## Q2 Prompt
I see. When you travel, do you prefer to plan everything in advance, or do you like to be spontaneous? Why?

## Q3 Prompt
Interesting. While some people choose to journey to distant and unfamiliar destinations, others feel more comfortable traveling closer to home. Which do you prefer, and why?

## Q4 Prompt
Good points. For my final question, in the future, do you think people will travel more frequently or less frequently? What factors do you think will influence this trend?

## Q1 My Polished Response
When I travel, my smartphone and my digital camera are two absolute essentials. My phone serves as my digital ticket holder, map, and translation tool, which keeps me organized and confident throughout the journey. Beyond that, carrying a dedicated camera allows me to capture high-quality photographs of local landscapes and architecture. Because I rely on these two versatile items, I can comfortably pack light and fit everything into a single carry-on bag, allowing me to navigate busy airports without lugging around heavy bags.

## Q2 My Polished Response
I definitely prefer planning my trips in advance rather than leaving things to chance. For one thing, booking accommodations and transit tickets early saves money and eliminates last-minute stress. On top of that, having a structured daily itinerary ensures that I do not miss out on key historical sights or cultural events. While leaving room for spontaneous discoveries can certainly be fun, having a solid plan gives me peace of mind and makes my overall travel experience much smoother.

## Q3 My Polished Response
I strongly prefer traveling to distant and unfamiliar destinations rather than staying close to home. Exploring new countries allows me to immerse myself in diverse cultures, sample authentic regional cuisine, and hear different languages. Although staying local is comfortable and convenient, venturing far abroad pushes me out of my comfort zone and significantly broadens my global perspective. That profound sense of discovery is ultimately what makes travel so transformative for me.

## Q4 My Polished Response
In the future, I believe people will travel much more frequently than they do today. The primary reason is that modern transportation is becoming faster, safer, and more affordable for everyday travelers. On top of that, the widespread adoption of remote work allows professionals to fulfill their job duties from anywhere in the world, granting them unprecedented geographical flexibility. As travel becomes increasingly accessible, more people will take advantage of the opportunity to explore international destinations.
