## Title
Attire and Clothing Choices

## Q1 Prompt
Thank you for your participation. I'd like to ask you about your clothing choices. What kinds of clothes do you usually wear in your daily life? Do you tend to dress for comfort or for appearance? Please explain.

## Q2 Prompt
Good. Can you recall a time when you wore something special for an event that was important to you? Please tell me about it and explain why you decided to wear that.

## Q3 Prompt
Interesting. Some schools require their students to wear uniforms every day. Do you agree or disagree that wearing uniforms in school is important for students? Why or why not?

## Q4 Prompt
Good point. For my final question, in your opinion, how important is it for people to choose appropriate clothes when meeting new people for the first time, such as at a job interview, and why?

## Q1 My Polished Response
In my daily life, I usually choose business casual clothing because it perfectly balances personal comfort with a professional appearance. First, maintaining a streamlined wardrobe of business casual attire makes getting ready in the morning remarkably fast and effortless, eliminating the stress of selecting an outfit. For instance, pairing a simple button-down shirt with tailored trousers allows me to start my day without delay. Second, these garments allow me to stay physically relaxed throughout long working hours while still presenting a tidy, respectable image to colleagues. As a result, this style offers the ideal compromise between practical ease and appropriate workplace standards. Therefore, business casual attire remains my primary choice for everyday wear.

## Q2 My Polished Response
I almost never wear formal suits in my daily routine, but I once had to wear one while competing as an oralist in a university moot court competition. At first, I felt uncomfortable with the strict dress code because formal suits do not align with my usual relaxed dressing habits. However, once I put on the tailored blazer, I immediately experienced a sudden surge of confidence and professional pride. Standing before the panel of judges in proper formal attire made the legal arguments feel far more solemn and impactful. Ultimately, wearing that suit enhanced my performance and gave me a deep sense of accomplishment during the event.

## Q3 My Polished Response
I strongly disagree with requiring students to wear school uniforms every day. For one thing, mandatory uniforms deprive young people of the freedom to choose clothing that aligns with their personal tastes and physical comfort. For example, when students are forced into rigid, identical outfits, they lose a valuable daily opportunity to experiment with fashion and build their own aesthetic philosophy. Furthermore, allowing students to select their own clothes fosters individuality and self-expression, which are crucial for healthy psychological development during school years. Therefore, schools should respect students' autonomy rather than enforcing a standardized dress code.

## Q4 My Polished Response
In my opinion, choosing appropriate attire when meeting people for the first time is essential because it sets a respectful tone for the relationship. While personal comfort is important in casual settings, professional situations like job interviews demand an outfit that demonstrates respect for the organization's standards. For instance, dressing professionally shows the interviewer that you take the opportunity seriously and possess strong professional discipline. Moreover, wearing suitable attire boosts your personal confidence, allowing you to perform at your absolute best during crucial conversations. Consequently, selecting the right clothes for a first meeting is vital for making a positive, lasting impression.
