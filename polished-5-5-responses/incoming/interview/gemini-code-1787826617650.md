## Q3 My Polished Response
I strongly agree that creative activities are valuable for everyone, regardless of whether they possess natural artistic talent. The primary reason is that creative expression fosters cognitive flexibility and emotional resilience, benefiting individuals in every profession. For instance, engaging in accessible hobbies like watercolor painting or reflective journaling helps people manage daily work stress and process their personal experiences in a constructive way. Therefore, artistic pursuits should be viewed as essential self-care rather than a privilege reserved for gifted artists.

## Q4 My Polished Response
In my opinion, children will unfortunately have significantly less time for genuine creative activities in the future. The fundamental issue lies in how modern digital platforms fragment developing attention spans and encourage passive media consumption. For example, social media applications and short-form video platforms are specifically engineered to maximize screen time by offering constant dopamine feedback, which leaves children with little cognitive space for imaginative play or artistic creation. Ultimately, without intentional screen-free boundaries, digital distractions will diminish childhood creativity.
