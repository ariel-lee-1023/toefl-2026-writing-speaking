## Q3 My Polished Response
I strongly believe that children should begin learning a second language at a very early age. Cognitive science demonstrates that young brains exhibit heightened neural plasticity, making it much easier for children to pick up native-like pronunciation and intuitive grammar rules effortlessly. While older learners may bring stronger analytical skills to formal study, starting early offers young children years of organic immersion, allowing the language to become second nature while enhancing their overall cognitive flexibility and problem-solving abilities.
