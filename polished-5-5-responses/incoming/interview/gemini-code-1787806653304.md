## Title
Celebrations and Special Occasions

## Q1 Prompt
Thank you for your participation. I'd like to ask you about celebrations and special occasions. What kinds of activities do people in your area usually do to celebrate? For example, do they prefer sharing meals, giving gifts, dancing, or other types?

## Q2 Prompt
I see. When you celebrate special occasions, do you prefer small celebrations with close family and friends, or large celebrations with many guests, and why?

## Q3 Prompt
Interesting. In recent years, many people have started sharing photos and videos of their celebrations on social media. Do you think this is a positive change? Why or why not?

## Q4 Prompt
Good points. For my final question, in the future, do you think traditional ways of celebrating special occasions will become more important to people, or do you think they will become less important? What factors do you think will influence this?

## Q1 My Polished Response
In my local area, the most popular way people celebrate social occasions is by gathering for a shared meal at a local restaurant. The primary reason is that enjoying exceptional food creates a lasting sensory connection to the milestone being marked. For instance, when my family celebrated my graduation at a neighborhood bistro, the memorable dishes made the evening feel truly distinctive. Plus, sitting down together offers a relaxed setting for family and friends to catch up without the stress of cooking or cleaning up afterward. Furthermore, restaurant dining is particularly conducive to extended conversations, allowing everyone to open up and build deeper emotional connections over the course of an evening. Ultimately, sharing food at a restaurant remains our favorite way to honor life's special moments together.

## Q2 My Polished Response
I strongly prefer small celebrations with an intimate circle of close family and friends. As an introvert, I find large gatherings mentally draining because navigating awkward small talk with strangers requires immense energy. By contrast, hosting a quiet dinner party with my inner circle allows me to engage in meaningful conversations with people who truly understand me. For example, during my last birthday, having just four close friends over led to deep, honest discussions about our future plans and shared hobbies. This kind of quality time feels far more fulfilling than wandering around a noisy venue packed with casual acquaintances. Therefore, celebrating in a intimate setting always yields the most memorable and meaningful experiences for me.

## Q3 My Polished Response
I believe that sharing celebration photos on social media represents a net negative trend in modern society. First, this practice encourages individuals to seek external validation through likes and comments rather than fully immersing themselves in the emotional moment. For instance, people often spend fifteen minutes setting up the perfect group photo at a party instead of enjoying the actual conversation. Second, it creates unhealthy pressure to curate a flawless public persona, which actively detracts from their genuine enjoyment with those around them. This shift turns personal milestones into performance content for public consumption rather than authentic private memories. Consequently, this trend distracts us from what truly matters during major celebrations.

## Q4 My Polished Response
In the future, I am convinced that traditional ways of celebrating special occasions will become even more important to people. For one thing, long-standing traditional customs reflect fundamental human needs for belonging, cultural identity, and continuity across generations. While modern digital trends change rapidly, timeless practices like family holiday gatherings provide a vital sense of stability that trendy events simply cannot replace. For example, even as digital tools evolve, people still travel long distances just to perform traditional annual rituals together in person. Although new celebration formats will certainly pop up online, the comforting root of heritage will keep traditional practices central to our lives. Thus, our cultural traditions will remain the bedrock of how we honor important milestones.
