## Title
Attitudes Toward Technology

## Q1 Prompt
Thank you for your participation. I'd like to ask you about your attitudes toward technology. What kinds of devices do you use most often in your daily life? For example, do you usually rely on phones, computers, tablets, or other types?

## Q2 Prompt
Great. When you need information about something new, do you prefer to search for it online, or do you find it more helpful to ask someone you know? Why?

## Q3 Prompt
Interesting. Some people believe young children should use phones and tablets to help them learn, while others think children should spend more time playing outside or reading. Which view do you agree with and why?

## Q4 Prompt
Good points. I just have one more question. Some people think new technology makes life easier and better, while others worry that it creates new problems. What do you think? Please explain.

## Q1 My Polished Response
The devices I rely on most in my daily life are my smartphone and my AirPods. My phone serves as my primary workstation, where I manage my daily calendar, track work requests, and practice key work tasks on the go. Second, my AirPods are essential because they allow me to listen to audio information and join calls without disturbing people around me.

## Q2 My Polished Response
When I need to learn about an unfamiliar topic, I prefer asking an AI assistant online rather than consulting an expert or someone I know. First, AI gives me immediate access to specialized knowledge that I would not otherwise have through my personal network. Second, I can ask endless follow-up questions until I fully understand the concept, knowing that the AI will never grow tired or impatient.

## Q3 My Polished Response
I believe schools should actively integrate digital devices into young children’s learning environments. First, interactive technology makes acquiring new information far more efficient and engaging for young minds. Second, early exposure to phones and tablets builds essential digital literacy, which children need to navigate our modern, tech-driven world successfully. Therefore, while outdoor play is valuable, incorporating technology into education is crucial.

## Q4 My Polished Response
In my view, new technology generally makes life better, even though it can sometimes amplify underlying human problems. On the positive side, modern tools allow us to access information and solve complex tasks far more efficiently, which enhances overall convenience and daily well-being. However, technology cannot fix existing personal issues, such as poor communication in personal relationships, and it often makes those existing friction points more obvious. Overall, as long as we address our personal habits, technology brings far more benefits than harms.
