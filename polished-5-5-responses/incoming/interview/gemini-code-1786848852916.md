I firmly believe that having pets is beneficial for children because it teaches them genuine empathy and responsibility. While parents might initially take on extra chores, establishing a daily routine turns pet care into a natural family habit rather than a burden. Ultimately, the developmental benefits for children far outweigh the added housework.
