## Title
Outdoor Activities Trends

## Q1 Prompt
Thank you for taking the time to speak with me. I'd like to ask you about outdoor activities. Are there any kinds of outdoor activities that are popular where you live? Like walking, biking, sports, gardening, or something else?

## Q2 Prompt
I see. Do you prefer outdoor activities that are slow and relaxing, like walking, or activities that are more physical and challenging? And why?

## Q3 Prompt
Interesting. In recent years, many cities have built more parks and outdoor spaces for their residents to enjoy. What do you think are the main reasons for this trend?

## Q4 Prompt
Good points. One final question. In the future, do you think people will spend more time outdoors than they do today, or do you think people will spend less time outdoors? Why?

## Q1 My Polished Response
In my local area, running is easily the most popular outdoor activity. There is a large public park near my home that features scenic mountain trails and paved paths designed specifically for local runners. First, running in this park allows residents to stay physically fit and improve their endurance. Second, it creates a welcoming community environment where neighbors can easily interact, chat, and share their running experiences.

## Q2 My Polished Response
I strongly prefer outdoor activities that are physically challenging over slow, relaxing ones. My favorite sport is soccer, which requires intense physical exertion and a high level of competitive energy. First, team-based competitive sports are far more engaging and exciting than solo exercises like walking. Second, participating in team sports gives me valuable social time, which helps me build strong relationships and make new friends.

## Q3 My Polished Response
The main reason city planners and developers are building more parks is to meet the growing resident demand for outdoor recreation space. First, these green spaces provide families with a safe environment where children can play and exercise together. Second, community parks serve as vital social hubs where neighbors can gather for public events, foster stronger community bonds, and escape the stress of city life.

## Q4 My Polished Response
In the future, I believe people will spend significantly less time outdoors, primarily due to the rising threat of extreme climate change. Unstable and uncomfortable weather patterns make outdoor exercise plans increasingly unreliable. As a result, people will prefer indoor activities, where they can work out in climate-controlled environments while conveniently watching their favorite television shows.

## My Diagnosis
- Run-on sentences and lack of connective structure: Q1 and Q2 relied heavily on long, merged chains of clauses connected repeatedly by "because... and... allow...", resulting in awkward pacing and reduced syntactic clarity.
- Imprecise vocabulary and ungrammatical prepositions: Used awkward or non-standard constructions such as "mountain and passes" (instead of "trails and paths"), "physical challenging", "discuss about", and "allow me to have relations with others and make friends with".
