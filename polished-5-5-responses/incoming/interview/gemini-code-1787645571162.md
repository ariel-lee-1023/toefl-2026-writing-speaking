## Q1 Prompt

## Q2 Prompt
I see. When you celebrate special occasions, do you prefer small celebrations with close family and friends, or large celebrations with many guests, and why?

## Q3 Prompt
Interesting. In recent years, many people have started sharing photos and videos of their celebrations on social media. Do you think this is a positive change? Why or why not?

## Q4 Prompt
Good points. For my final question, in the future, do you think traditional ways of celebrating special occasions will become more important to people, or do you think they will become less important? What factors do you think will influence this?

## Q1 My Polished Response
In my local area, the most popular way people celebrate special occasions is by gathering for a delicious meal at a nice restaurant. First, enjoying great food creates a memorable connection between the event and a positive sensory experience. Second, sharing a quality meal gives families and friends a relaxed environment to communicate, catch up, and enjoy each other's company in a pleasant mood.

## Q2 My Polished Response
When celebrating special occasions, I strongly prefer small gatherings with close family and friends over large parties. First, as an introvert, I find large events exhausting because they require extra effort to socialize with strangers. Second, celebrating with an intimate circle allows me to share happy moments with the people who truly know and understand me best.

## Q3 My Polished Response
I view the trend of sharing celebration photos on social media as a negative change. First, it encourages people to seek external validation rather than focusing on their genuine internal feelings during special moments. Second, it creates unhealthy pressure to curate a perfect public image, which distracts people from actually enjoying the celebration with those around them.

## Q4 My Polished Response
In the future, I believe traditional ways of celebrating will become even more important to people. First, traditional customs fulfill a fundamental human need for connection, sense of belonging, and continuity across generations. Second, because these traditions are deeply rooted in our upbringing and cultural habits, they provide a sense of stability that modern, fast-changing celebration trends simply cannot replace.
