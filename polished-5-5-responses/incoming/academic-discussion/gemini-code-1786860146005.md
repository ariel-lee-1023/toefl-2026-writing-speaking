## Title
Globalization and Local Economies

## Prompt
Dr. Gupta
This week, we have been exploring the effects of globalization on local economies. Some economists argue that globalization creates new markets, attracts investment, and supports economic growth. Others believe that it harms local businesses and causes job losses when companies move production to places with cheaper labor. Do you think globalization has a positive or negative impact on local economies? Why?

Andrew
Globalization gives local companies access to more customers, investment, technology, and specialized products.

Kelly
Global competition can force small businesses to close and may encourage companies to move jobs away from local communities.

## My Polished Response
I believe globalization can have a positive impact on local economies, but only when governments help workers and small businesses adapt. Access to international markets allows a local company to sell beyond its region, purchase specialized equipment, and learn more efficient production methods. Foreign investment can also create jobs and improve transportation or communication networks. However, Kelly correctly notes that the benefits are not distributed automatically. A factory may close when a company moves production abroad, and small stores may struggle against large international competitors. Communities that depend on one industry can suffer serious short-term losses. Governments can fund retraining for displaced workers, provide loans and digital tools to small businesses, and require foreign investors to follow labor and environmental standards.
