## Title
Minimum Wage Policy Debate

## Prompt
Dr. Sharma
We've been discussing the debate over minimum wage policies. The minimum wage is the lowest hourly pay that employers are legally required to offer their workers. Some economists argue that raising the minimum wage helps reduce poverty and allows workers to meet their basic needs. Others believe that higher minimum wages can lead to job losses, as businesses may not be able to afford increased labor costs. What is your opinion on this issue?

Jordan
I think raising the minimum wage is important. Many workers struggle to cover basic expenses like rent, food, and transportation on their current pay. A higher minimum wage would give people more financial stability and allow them to live with greater dignity. It's only fair that hard work should be enough to support a decent standard of living.

Sam
I believe raising the minimum wage too much can actually hurt the people it's meant to help. Small businesses may be forced to cut hours or lay off workers if they can't afford higher wages. In some cases, it could even lead to businesses closing altogether, which would reduce job opportunities in the community.

## My Polished Response
While I understand Sam's concern that a higher minimum wage might increase operational costs for businesses, I agree with Jordan that raising the minimum wage is crucial for allowing workers to live with greater dignity. What is more, I believe raising the minimum wage could ultimately strengthen the overall economy by boosting consumer spending. When low-income workers earn higher pay, they do not simply hoard the extra income; instead, they immediately spend it on necessities and local services, directly driving revenue back into local businesses. For example, when wage floors were raised in several urban centers, increased consumer demand prompted companies to expand hiring rather than cut jobs, thereby offsetting initial labor expenses. Today, a guaranteed minimum wage ensures that companies share their profit growth with the workforce, creating a far more sustainable economic environment for everyone involved.
