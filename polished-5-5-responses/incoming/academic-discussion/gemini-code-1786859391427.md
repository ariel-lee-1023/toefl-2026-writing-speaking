## Title
Climate Education Focus

## Prompt
Dr. Achebe
Schools need to prepare students for climate change and other environmental problems. Education focused on science and technology can help students develop innovative solutions. Education emphasizing social sciences and ethics can foster a deeper understanding of environmental problems and their effects. Which approach best prepares students to face these challenges?

Claire
Social sciences help students understand global communities and the need for collective action.

Andrew
Technical skills provide practical solutions to problems such as renewable energy and waste reduction.

## My Polished Response
I agree with Andrew that science and technology education prepares students more directly for climate change. Ethical awareness is important, but society also needs people who can design and carry out workable solutions. First, technical courses give students tools for reducing emissions. In chemistry or engineering classes, students can learn how batteries store renewable energy, how buildings conserve heat, and how waste can be reused. A student who understands these systems can later improve them as a researcher, engineer, or city planner. This creates measurable environmental benefits rather than awareness alone. Second, scientific training helps students evaluate claims accurately. Climate debates often include misleading statistics about energy costs or pollution. Students who can interpret data are better able to compare policies and identify solutions that produce real results. They can also explain those results clearly to the public. Claire is right that social sciences encourage cooperation and help students understand fairness. However, without technical knowledge, collective action risks being directed toward inefficient or counterproductive policies. When citizens understand the mechanics of green technology, their cooperative efforts become far more impactful because they can advocate for solutions that are scientifically sound. Ultimately, while ethical awareness inspires people to care about the planet, technical education provides the practical tools required to save it.
