# Pharmacy Prescription Dispensing

## Prompt
Scenario: a pharmacy counter dispensing prescriptions to customers

1. Start by asking the customer for their name.
2. Then look up their prescription in the system.
3. Get the labelled bag from the shelf to verify.
4. Check the dosage on the medication label.
5. Explain the dosing instructions before taking payment.
6. If a customer wants more refills, you can order them through the system.
7. Be sure to point out any allergy warnings on the bottle to the customer.

## My Chunking & Memory Strategy
Short sentences are pure action-plus-place or action-plus-purpose and can be held as one or two chunks. Medium sentences each add one quiet function-word chunk (*from the shelf, on the medication label, before taking payment*). The two long sentences decide the set: sentence 6 splits cleanly at the comma after the *if* condition, then adds a softened *you can* action and a place chunk; sentence 7 is a softened command whose *on the bottle* and *to the customer* are free place and purpose boundaries. Reconstruct any missing function word from the chunk type. Never rebuild the sentence from your own grammar; the chunks exist only to keep the speaker’s exact wording long enough to finish.

## My Pronunciation Focus
- Compressed function words: their / the / in / on / from / to / for / before / through / any — each recoverable from its chunk type (place → in/on/from/through, purpose → to/for, time → before).
- Word endings (-s / -ed / final t-d): prescription, labelled, dosage, dosing, instructions, refills, warnings, bottle, customer. Content words that carry no ending (look, ask, get, check, explain, order, point) must stay clean.
- Rhythm & stress: imitate the speaker’s stress on the content words and the slight pause after the *if* clause or after *Be sure to*; do not substitute your own conversational rhythm.
- Content words to say crisply: prescription, labelled, dosage, dosing, instructions, refills, allergy, warnings — any blur produces the ambiguity the rubric penalizes.
