# Photography Studio Setup

## Prompt
Scenario: A photography instructor guiding you through studio setup.

1. Begin with the camera mounted on its tripod.
2. Attach the lens to the camera body.
3. Turn on the studio light to warm the bulb.
4. Open the white umbrella beside the studio light.
5. Place the white reflector beside the chair before the shoot starts.
6. You should hang a clean backdrop behind the subject before each portrait session begins.

## My Chunking & Memory Strategy
Focus on identifying functional chunk markers to hold 2–3 ideas rather than individual words. Sentences 4 and 5 share the spatial place marker "beside the...", while Sentences 5 and 6 rely on time markers ("before the shoot starts", "before each portrait session begins"). Recognizing these repeating structural anchors frees up cognitive space to retain full sentences without truncating the final chunks.

## My Pronunciation Focus
- Compressed function words: Prepositions linking place and purpose chunks (attach *to*, *with* the camera, *beside* the light, *behind* the subject).
- Word endings (-s / -ed / final t-d): Deliberate focus on past participle *-ed* in *mounted*, third-person *-s* in *starts* and *begins*, and distinguishing singular *light* and *shoot* from plural/gerund variations.
- Rhythm & stress: Maintain natural English cadence by stressing key nouns (camera, tripod, lens, reflector, backdrop) while keeping function words light and connected.
- Content words to say crisply: *mounted*, *backdrop*, *reflector*, *portrait session*.
