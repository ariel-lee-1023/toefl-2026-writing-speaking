# Before They Leave Thank Customers Business

## Prompt
1. Before they leave, thank customers for their business and assist them as needed.
2. Scan the barcode for each item on the belt.
3. You can also enter prices by hand.
4. Print the receipt for the customer by request only.
5. Use the card reading machine for all electronic payments.
6. Verify the amount before completing the transaction.
7. Make sure to keep the checkout area organized, well stocked and clean at all times.

## My Chunking & Memory Strategy
- Chunk 1 (Time/Condition): Before they leave / when entering / before completing
- Chunk 2 (Action): thank customers / scan the barcode / enter prices / print the receipt / use the machine / verify the amount / keep the area
- Chunk 3 (Object/Purpose/Place): for their business / for each item on the belt / by hand / for the customer by request only / for all electronic payments / organized, well stocked and clean at all times

## My Pronunciation Focus
- Compressed function words: for, on, by, as, to, at, an, the
- Word endings (-s/-ed, unreleased final consonants): customers, prices, receipt, payments, organized, stocked, times
- Rhythm & intonation: Stressed action verbs and key nouns with reduced, rapid linkings on prepositions and articles

## My Self-Assessment
- Score: 4/5. Solid capture of main commands, but lost precision on compressed prepositions and word endings like plural s and past participle ed. Next time, use chunk types to predict function words and focus on crisp final consonant delivery.
