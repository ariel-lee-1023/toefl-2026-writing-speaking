# Community Center Tours

## Prompt
Scenario: You are volunteering at a community center for course credit. A leader is training you how to give tours to community members. Listen to the leader and repeat what the leader says. Repeat only once.

1. Welcome to the community center.
2. Go to the information desk for help.
3. The activity rooms hold various classes daily.
4. The gym is where our popular fitness programs are held.
5. We recommend a visit to the cafe for a snack or drink.
6. We have a notice board for community events and important announcements.
7. We have free flyers if anyone is interested in our new courses.

## My Chunking & Memory Strategy
The opening welcome is only five words; it should not be expanded to fit a predicted short-sentence range. Item 3 is a statement about rooms, so its subject belongs in the retained text rather than being converted into an instruction. In item 4, "where our popular fitness programs are held" describes the gym; keep the passive are held inside that unit. Item 5 groups the recommendation, the cafe destination, and the refreshment purpose. Item 6 coordinates events and announcements within the same for-phrase, rather than issuing two actions. Item 7 places the if-condition after the main statement; "in our new courses" completes interested. Retaining these real relationships is more useful than forcing every longer item into an imperative pattern. Word counts treat contractions and hyphenated compounds as one word. ## My Pronunciation Focus
- Compressed function words: Destination information takes "to the community center", "to the information desk", and "to the cafe". Purpose takes "for help", "for a snack or drink", and "for community events". Or presents the refreshment alternatives, while and joins events to announcements. The last statement needs the sequence "if anyone is interested in our new courses", including is and in.
- Word endings (-s / -ed / final t-d): Rooms, classes, programs, events, announcements, flyers, and courses retain their plural endings. Interested retains -ed. Hold, held, recommend, and board end in d; visit and important end in t. Center, desk, activity, gym, notice, cafe, snack, and drink do not gain a plural ending. The ss in fitness belongs to the word itself. Listen to each ending because chunk type alone cannot decide its form.
- Rhythm & stress: No audio was supplied, so the speaker's stress pattern and any rhythm substitution cannot be identified. The following are text-based phrasing suggestions only: Keep "are held" attached to programs in item 4. In item 7, carry the statement through "in our new courses" so the interest phrase remains complete.
- Content words to say crisply: community, information, various, classes, fitness, programs, recommend, announcements, flyers, courses.
