# Pet Clinic Check In

## Prompt
1. Begin by greeting each pet owner warmly.
2. Have them check in on the front tablet.
3. Place each pet on the scale to record weight.
4. Direct the owners to seats in the waiting area.
5. Watch the screen for the next exam room number.
6. You can offer each pet a small treat from the jar after their appointment.
7. Make sure to keep every dog on a leash whenever they are inside the clinic.

## My Chunking & Memory Strategy
1 short: action → greeting each pet owner · manner → warmly
2 short: action → have them check in · place → on the front tablet
3 medium: action → place each pet · place → on the scale · purpose → to record weight
4 medium: action → direct the owners · place → to seats in the waiting area
5 medium: action → watch the screen · purpose → for the next exam room number
6 long softened+time: action → offer each pet a small treat · place → from the jar · time → after their appointment
7 long softened+time: action → keep every dog on a leash · time → whenever they are inside the clinic
Function-word reconstruction: place → on/in; purpose → to/for; time → after/whenever. Endings cannot be predicted; attend to owners, seats, pets, treats, dogs.

## My Pronunciation Focus
- Compressed function words: on (place), to (purpose), after/whenever (time)
- Word endings (-s / -ed / final t-d): owners, seats, pets, treats, dogs, leash; final t in treat
- Rhythm & stress: imitate the speaker’s stress on the content verbs and the final time clauses
- Content words to say crisply: tablet, scale, screen, jar, clinic
