# Mixed Grammar & Linking Practice

## Sentences
1. The committee will announce its decision by the end of the month.
2. Despite the heavy rain, the construction crew continued working on the bridge.
3. She's been studying abroad for nearly three years now.
4. If I had known about the traffic, I would have left earlier.
5. The museum's new exhibit features artifacts from ancient civilizations.

## Difficulty Notes
- **Sentence 2:** Tricky linking between "heavy" and "rain" (*heavy-rain* runs together); the unstressed "the" before "construction" is easily swallowed — keep it reduced but audible.
- **Sentence 3:** The *have been -ing* stress pattern (*she's BEEN STUDying*) is easy to flatten — make sure "been" carries noticeable length and the "-ing" verb gets primary stress on the first syllable.
- **Sentence 4:** Third conditional structure ("If I had known… I would have left…") with the contracted "I'd've" cluster — easy to rush; slow down and let each auxiliary land clearly.

## Self-Assessment
- Sentences 1 and 5: Felt comfortable; no major issues reported.
- Sentence 2: Struggled with the "heavy rain" linking — needs more focused drilling.
- Sentence 3: The *been -ing* stress pattern is a recurring weak point; needs deliberate practice with exaggerated stress before returning to natural speed.
- Sentence 4: Tendency to rush through the third conditional contraction ("I'd have" / "I'd've") — practice at slow tempo first, then build up speed.
