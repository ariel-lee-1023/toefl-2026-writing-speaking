# Gym Equipment Orientation

## Prompt
Scenario: a gym or fitness-center orientation on equipment and safe use

1. Begin with a warm-up on the treadmill.
2. Use the weights for strength training exercises.
3. You can adjust the bench for support during your workouts.
4. Do stretching exercises on the mat to prevent injury.
5. Wipe down equipment after use to maintain good hygiene.
6. Please read and follow the instructions for each machine carefully to avoid injury.
7. Take breaks as needed, and be sure to stay hydrated to obtain the best results.

## My Chunking & Memory Strategy
Short sentences are pure action-plus-place or action-plus-purpose and can be held as one or two chunks. Medium sentences each add one quiet function-word chunk (*for support during your workouts, on the mat to prevent, after use to maintain*). The two long sentences decide the set: sentence 6 is an and-serial inside a polite command whose final *to avoid* is a free purpose boundary; sentence 7 is an and-serial whose second half is itself softened, with the comma after *needed* as a free boundary. Reconstruct any missing function word from the chunk type. Never rebuild the sentence from your own grammar; the chunks exist only to keep the speaker’s exact wording long enough to finish.

## My Pronunciation Focus
- Compressed function words: with / a / on / the / for / you / can / during / your / to / after / and / each / as — each recoverable from its chunk type (place → on, purpose → for/to, time → during/after, serial → and).
- Word endings (-s / -ed / final t-d): warm-up, treadmill, weights, exercises, bench, support, workouts, stretching, exercises, mat, injury, equipment, hygiene, instructions, machine, injury, breaks, results. Content words that carry no ending (begin, use, adjust, do, wipe, read, follow, take, stay, obtain) must stay clean.
- Rhythm & stress: imitate the speaker’s stress on the content words and the slight pause after the *and* or before the final purpose chunk; do not substitute your own conversational rhythm.
- Content words to say crisply: warm-up, treadmill, strength, stretching, hygiene, instructions, hydrated, results — any blur produces the ambiguity the rubric penalizes.
