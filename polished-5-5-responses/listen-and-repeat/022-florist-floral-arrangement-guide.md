# Florist Floral Arrangement Guide

## Prompt
Scenario: A supervisor guiding a new employee on how to prepare a custom bouquet for a customer.

1. First, ask the customer about the occasion.
2. Show them the buckets of fresh roses.
3. Pick three or four stems for a small bouquet.
4. Add greenery between the flowers for a fuller look.
5. Trim the bottom stems to even the length.
6. You can wrap the bundle in brown paper and tie a ribbon at the base.
7. After wrapping the bouquet, hand it to the customer with both hands and a smile.

## My Chunking & Memory Strategy
Boundaries follow natural category transitions (action → object → place/purpose/manner). In sentence #6, the conjunction "and" marks a clean split between two action chunks. In sentence #7, the introductory time chunk ("After wrapping the bouquet") provides a natural pause boundary that isolates the main command and manner phrases.
