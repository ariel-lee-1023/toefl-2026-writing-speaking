# Food Station Procedures

## Prompt
Scenario: a supervisor training you at a food service station

1. Put on clean gloves before you handle food.
2. Check that food is at the right temperature.
3. Keep dishes covered at your station during your shift.
4. Watch food levels at your station and ask for more as needed.
5. Clean your workstation, often during your shift to keep it tidy.
6. To keep your workspace organized, store items on the correct shelf and grouped by type.
7. If you see a safety issue, report it right away so it can be addressed.

## My Chunking & Memory Strategy
I chunked the short sentences into simple action plus time or place combinations (e.g., action → Put on clean gloves · time → before you handle food). The long 15-word Sentence 6 uses a front-loaded purpose chunk cued by "To," followed by an and-serial shape, requiring careful boundary pauses after "organized" and "shelf." Sentence 7 utilizes a conditional if-then shape cued by "If," with a natural boundary before the purpose chunk "so it can be addressed." This anticipates the structure to prevent mid-sentence hesitation.

## My Pronunciation Focus
- Compressed function words: time chunks predicting "before" and "during", place chunks predicting "at" and "on", and purpose chunks predicting "to" and "so".
- Word endings (-s / -ed / final t-d): gloves, dishes, covered, levels, items, organized, grouped, addressed. Content words that carry no ending and must not be pluralized include food, shift, and workstation.
- Rhythm & stress: Maintain forward momentum to avoid truncation on the 15-word sentences (Sentences 6 and 7). Finishing an imperfect sentence beats stopping to fix a word ending.
- Content words to say crisply: temperature, workspace, safety, addressed.
