# Course Registration System

## Prompt
Scenario: a student working through the online course-registration system

1. Start by searching for your desired courses.
2. Add the selections to your current schedule.
3. Be sure to review the registration rules carefully.
4. Check for any problems or errors before finalizing.
5. Determine if you made the right choices and complete the process.
6. If you encounter issues, you may use the support chat for any assistance you need.
7. Remember to log out of the system once you have received the confirmation message.

## My Chunking & Memory Strategy
The set opens with two pure action-plus-object/place commands that can be held as one or two chunks. Medium sentences each add one quiet function-word chunk (to-review, before-finalizing, if-you-made). The two long sentences decide the score: sentence 6 is an if-condition plus a softened command plus a purpose chunk; sentence 7 is a softened command plus a front-loaded time chunk whose *once* supplies a free boundary. Reconstruct any missing function word from the chunk type rather than trying to remember the compressed sound. Never rebuild from your own grammar; the chunks exist only to keep the speaker’s exact wording in memory long enough to finish the sentence.

## My Pronunciation Focus
- Compressed function words: the (selections / registration / system / confirmation), to (review / log out), for (assistance), before / once, any / or — each recoverable from its chunk type (place → to/of, purpose → for, time → before/once).
- Word endings (-s / -ed / final t-d): selections, courses, rules, problems, errors, choices, issues, assistance, system, received, confirmation, message. Content words that carry no ending (search, add, review, check, determine, complete, encounter, use, log, remember) must stay clean; inventing an ending costs the same as dropping one.
- Rhythm & stress: imitate the speaker’s stress on the content words and the slight pause after the front-loaded *once* or the *and*; do not substitute your own conversational rhythm.
- Content words to say crisply: selections, registration, finalizing, choices, encounter, assistance, confirmation — any blur here produces ambiguity the rubric penalizes.
