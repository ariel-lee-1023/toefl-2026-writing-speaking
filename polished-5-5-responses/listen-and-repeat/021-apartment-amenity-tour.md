# Apartment Amenity Tour

## Prompt
Scenario: A supervisor training you on how to guide new residents through a building's shared amenities.

1. Start by showing them the front lobby.
2. Point out the mail and package room nearby.
3. Show them the gym for any fitness needs.
4. Walk them through the lounge on the second floor.
5. Take them up to the rooftop garden for fresh air.
6. Residents can find a list of events that the building hosts on the lobby bulletin board.
7. You will need to swipe your keycard to enter most of the shared amenities.

## My Chunking & Memory Strategy
Boundary points fall naturally along functional category transitions (action → object → place/purpose). In long sentences like #6 and #7, identifying structural markers—such as the relative pronoun "that" in #6 or the purpose infinitive "to enter" in #7—creates natural chunk breaks that protect against lexical substitution during spoken recall.

## My Pronunciation Focus
- Compressed function words: "that the" in relative clause chunks; "to" / "for" in purpose chunks; "on" in location chunks.
- Word endings (-s / -ed / final t-d): Residents, events, hosts, needs, amenities. Ensure content words like "garden" or "lounge" carry no false -s endings.
- Rhythm & stress: Maintain primary stress on key nouns ("rooftop garden", "bulletin board", "second floor", "keycard", "amenities").
- Content words to say crisply: bulletin board, rooftop, keycard, amenities.
