# Adjusting University City

## Prompt
Your friend Emma has recently moved to a new city to attend university and is feeling a bit overwhelmed. She has reached out to you for advice on how to adjust to her new environment and make new friends. You have lived in several cities before, and you have experience with finding ways to settle into new places. Write an email to Emma. In your email, do the following: Describe how you adjusted to a new city. Give Emma suggestions on how she can make new friends. Offer different ways to help her if she needs more advice.

## My Polished Response
Dear Emma,

I completely understand why you are feeling overwhelmed in an unfamiliar city, as I experienced several of the exact same mental struggles when I first moved. It always takes time to adapt, and I found that building a small, predictable daily routine really helped alleviate my initial sense of loneliness.

First, since you enjoy films, I highly recommend joining a university movie-watching club or attending local theater screenings. Watching movies together is a fantastic way to make new friends naturally because you instantly have a shared experience to discuss, giving you an easy chance to exchange views without the awkward pressure of forcing small talk.

Finally, please let me know if you want to schedule a video call this weekend just to vent, or if you would like me to help you research some interesting student organizations on your campus.

Warmly,

Ariel
