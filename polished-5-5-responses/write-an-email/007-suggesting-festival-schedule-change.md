# Suggesting Festival Schedule Change

## Prompt
You are a member of your neighborhood's cultural association. The association hosts a monthly international food festival on Saturday afternoons. However, many members, including yourself, have recently found it difficult to attend on Saturdays due to work commitments. You would like to suggest a schedule change.
Write an email to the association president, Mr. Volkov. In your email, do the following:

* Explain why you and other members have difficulty attending.

* Propose an alternative day and time for the festival.

* Offer to help communicate the change to other members.

Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

## My Polished Response
Dear Mr. Volkov,

I am writing to suggest a schedule change for our neighborhood’s monthly international food festival. I have really enjoyed these events, but recently many members, including myself, have found it difficult to attend on Saturday afternoons because of work commitments that often extend into the weekend.

To make it easier for more neighbors to participate, I would like to propose moving the festival to Sunday evenings from 5:00 p.m. to 7:00 p.m. This time would allow people who work on Saturdays or during Sunday daytime hours to join, share their dishes, and enjoy the gathering without conflicting with their employment schedules.

I would be happy to help communicate the change to other association members. I can draft a short update for our newsletter or post the new details on our community board and group chat if that would be useful.

Please let me know what you think of this suggestion. Thank you for your continued efforts in organizing these events.

Best regards,  
Ariel Lee

## My Diagnosis
The draft is a Score 4. It covers all three required actions with adequate elaboration: the attendance difficulty is stated, a concrete alternative time is proposed, and an offer of help is made. The tone is polite and the overall structure is appropriate. The ceiling is created by a less convincing personal reason (“mutual movie watch plan”) that does not align closely with the prompt’s emphasis on work commitments, several mechanical and spacing errors (“Iwould,” “thatinvolves,” missing spaces after commas), and an incomplete final sentence that weakens closure and clarity. These issues prevent the consistent precision and fully effective elaboration expected at Score 5.

## My What Changed & Why
The polished version retains the same three-part structure and helpful tone while raising the response to Score 5 level. The explanation of attendance difficulty is rewritten to center on work commitments, matching the prompt more closely and sounding more compelling. The alternative time (Sunday 5:00–7:00 p.m.) is kept, and the offer of help is completed and slightly expanded with concrete options (newsletter update, community board, group chat). All mechanical and spacing errors are corrected, every sentence is finished, and a brief courteous invitation for feedback is added to strengthen social conventions and closure.
