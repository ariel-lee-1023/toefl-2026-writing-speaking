# Animal Shelter Volunteer Application

## Prompt
Your friend told you that a local animal shelter is looking for volunteers to help with various tasks, such as taking care of dogs and cats, keeping records of adoption requests, and handling social media accounts. You would like to apply.
Write an email to Mr. Young, president of the animal shelter. In your email, do the following.

Explain why you want to volunteer.
Describe your qualifications.
State your preferred working days and times.
Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

## My Polished Response
Dear Mr. Young,
I would like to join your shelter as a volunteer, and this work is very suitable for me because of my rural experience, which gave me a deep love for cats and dogs and comprehensive knowledge of taking care of them.
Before attending college, I lived in my grandfather's house in the countryside, and he adopted many dogs and cats. I considered them my family members and friends, and I learned how to feed them, clean their spaces, and take care of them patiently. I can also help with simple record keeping if needed. It is quite sad for me to live without cats and dogs in recent years, so I really want to help them.
If you permit, I prefer to work on Mondays and Fridays from 2:00 to 6:00 p.m. per week.
Thank you for your time. 

Sincerely, 
Ariel Lee
