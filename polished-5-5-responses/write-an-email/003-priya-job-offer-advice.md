# Priya Job Offer Advice

## Prompt
Your friend Priya recently graduated from university and has received two job offers. One is at a large corporation with a higher salary, and the other is at a small startup closer to her family. She is having difficulty deciding and has asked for your advice.
Write an email to Priya. In your email, do the following:

* Describe the advantages of each option.

* Share your recommendation and explain your reasoning.

* Suggest how she might make her final decision.

Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

## My Polished Response
Dear Priya,

Congratulations on the two offers—that is a great position to be in after graduation.

The large corporation gives you a higher salary right away and the chance to work with experienced teams on established projects, which can accelerate your skill development and look strong on a résumé. The startup, on the other hand, is closer to your family, so you would have more regular support and less commuting stress; smaller companies also often let new employees take on wider responsibilities and see the direct impact of their work.

My recommendation is to lean toward the large corporation for the first few years. The higher pay and structured learning environment will give you a solid foundation and more options later, whether you stay or eventually move to a smaller company.

To decide, try making a simple weighted list: write down the three things that matter most to you right now (salary, family time, learning opportunities, for example), assign each a score from 1 to 5 for both jobs, and see which total feels more convincing. You could also visit both offices for a day if possible or talk with one current employee at each place.

Whatever you choose, I am confident it will work out. Let me know how the decision goes.

Best,  
Alex
