# Club Meeting Schedule Change

## Prompt
Cooking Club Schedule
You are a member of a student cooking club. You enjoy the club's activities, but the weekly meeting is currently held on Wednesday nights, which is a problem for you. You need to contact the club president, Lily, and request a change to the meeting schedule.
Write an email to Lily. In your email, do the following:
* Compliment Lily on the job she is doing as club president.
* Explain why the current weekly meeting schedule is a problem for you.
* Request a change to the meeting schedule going forward.

## My Polished Response
Dear Lily,
I hope you are well. First, I want to thank you for doing such a great job as club president. The recent bread-making workshop was well organized, and I appreciate how you encourage new members to participate.
Unfortunately, the Wednesday evening meeting time has become difficult for me. I have a required laboratory class that now ends at 7:30 p.m., so I usually arrive late and miss the recipe demonstration. Two other members in my class have the same conflict. Would it be possible to move our regular meeting to Tuesday evening or Saturday afternoon? Before changing the schedule, you could send a short survey with three possible times and select the option that works for the largest number of members. We could also record demonstrations for anyone who occasionally cannot attend.
Thank you for considering this request. I look forward to the next club activity.
Best,
Alex Lee
