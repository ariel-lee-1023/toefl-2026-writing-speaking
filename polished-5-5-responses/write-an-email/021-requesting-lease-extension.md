# Requesting Lease Extension

## Prompt
You have been renting an apartment from Mrs. Nakamura for the past two years. Your lease is ending next month, but you have not yet found a new place to live. You would like to ask for a short extension of your current lease.
Write an email to Mrs. Nakamura. In your email, do the following:

Explain why you need to extend your lease.
Describe your plan for finding a new apartment.
Propose a specific length for the extension.
Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

## My Polished Response
Dear Mrs. Nakamura,

Could I extend my lease for Apartment 3B by one month beyond its current end date? Unexpected work travel has limited my time for apartment hunting, and I have not yet found a suitable place.

I have scheduled viewings for the next two weekends and plan to submit an application by the end of this month. The extra month would give me time to complete the rental arrangements and organize my move.

I would be happy to sign a short extension agreement and continue paying rent as usual. Please let me know whether this would work for you.

Thank you for considering my request.

Best regards,
Ariel Lee
