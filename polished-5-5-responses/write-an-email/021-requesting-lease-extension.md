# Requesting Lease Extension

## Prompt
You have been renting an apartment from Mrs. Nakamura for the past two years. Your lease is ending next month, but you have not yet found a new place to live. You would like to ask for a short extension of your current lease.
Write an email to Mrs. Nakamura. In your email, do the following:

Explain why you need to extend your lease.
Describe your plan for finding a new apartment.
Propose a specific length for the extension.
Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

## My Polished Response
Dear Mrs. Nakamura,
I hope you are having a wonderful week. I am writing to ask if it might be possible to request a short extension of my current lease for Apartment 3B, which is set to end next month. I have thoroughly enjoyed living here over the past two years, but my recent job search and unexpected work travel have significantly delayed my ability to secure a new apartment before my move-out date.
To ensure that I transition out as smoothly as possible, my plan is to attend several scheduled apartment viewings over the next two weekends and finalize an application with a new landlord by the end of this month. Once an application is approved, I will immediately begin packing and organizing my move so that everything is completed efficiently.
Given this timeline, I would like to propose a one-month extension of my lease, which would allow me to stay through the end of next month. This additional time would give me the necessary window to complete my search and move without any disruption. Please let me know if this arrangement works for you, and I would be happy to sign a temporary agreement at your earliest convenience.
Thank you very much for your time, understanding, and continued kindness as a landlord.

Best regards,
Ariel Lee
