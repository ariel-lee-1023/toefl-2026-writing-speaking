# Heating System Maintenance Request

## Prompt
You live in a university dormitory managed by the campus housing office. For the past week, the heating system in your room has not been working properly, and the temperature drops significantly at night. You have tried adjusting the thermostat, but the problem continues.
Write an email to Ms. Dubois, the housing coordinator. In your email, do the following:
- Describe the problem with the heating system.
- Explain how it has affected your daily routine.
- Request a specific time for a maintenance visit.
Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

## My Polished Response
Dear Ms. Dubois,

I am writing to report an ongoing issue with the heating system in room 304 of West Hall. Over the past week, our room's temperature has remained consistently below 20°C, and the thermostat drops significantly every night despite our efforts to adjust it.

This cold environment has severely disrupted our daily routine, making it difficult to get a full night's sleep and leaving us too exhausted to focus on our morning studies. Since adjusting the controls manually has not resolved the problem, we require assistance from the maintenance personnel.

Could you please send a technician to inspect the system tomorrow, Thursday, between 2:00 p.m. and 4:00 p.m., when one of us will be present in the room? Thank you for your time and assistance with this matter.

Sincerely,
Alex Lee

## My Draft
Dear Ms.Dubois,
On behalf of our room, I am writing to report the widespread heating issues affecting multiple dorm rooms recently. Our room's temperature has been consistently below 20°C for an extended period, and it is something we cannot fix ourselves.
The uncomfortable temperature in the room has been disrupting our sleep and negatively impacting our daytime productivity.
Could you please let us know when a repair team might be able to come by? Thank you for your time and help, and I wish you all the best with your work.
Sincerely, Alex Lee

## My Diagnosis
- Incomplete Request Specificity: The prompt specifically required requesting a specific time for a maintenance visit, but the draft asked a broad open question ("Could you please let us know when a repair team might be able to come by?").
- Broad / Unsupported Generalities: Asserting "widespread heating issues affecting multiple dorm rooms" without specific context or proof weakened the focus and realism of the personal campus scenario.
- Typographical and Formatting Errors: Missing space after punctuation in the salutation ("Ms.Dubois") and lack of paragraph breaks separating the distinct prompt actions.

## My What Changed & Why
- Action Formulation: Replaced the generic inquiry with a concrete date and time window ("tomorrow, Thursday, between 2:00 p.m. and 4:00 p.m.") to fully satisfy the third prompt bullet on request formulation.
- Grounded Details: Shifted focus from broad campus claims to room-specific details (room 304 of West Hall, manual thermostat attempts) to increase concrete elaboration.
- Syntactic Variety: Integrated causal and conditional dependent clauses ("Since adjusting the controls manually...", "when one of us will be present...") to demonstrate advanced sentence control and precision.
