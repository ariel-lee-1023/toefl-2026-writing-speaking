# Health During Assignment Deadlines

## Prompt
Background:
You have noticed that your friend Alex has been staying up late to finish his assignments and seems to be ignoring his health.

Requirements:
1. Mention that you have noticed he has been sacrificing his health to complete his schoolwork
2. Explain why maintaining good health is important
3. Give some suggestions on how he could reduce study stress and take better care of himself

## My Polished Response
Hi Alex,

I've noticed you've been staying up late to finish assignments, skipping meals, and looking exhausted in class. I know you're working hard, but I'm worried schoolwork is leaving you too little time to look after yourself.

Getting enough rest and eating regularly can help you stay focused and make better use of your study time. When you're exhausted, even familiar tasks can take longer, making deadlines feel more stressful.

Maybe you could prioritize your weekly assignments and set a realistic stopping time each evening. Try to leave room for regular meals and short breaks, too. If several deadlines overlap, it might help to discuss your workload with your professors. I'd be happy to plan a manageable study schedule with you.

Best,
Ariel
