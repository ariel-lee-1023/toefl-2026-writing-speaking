# Study Group First Meeting

## Prompt
Study Group First Meeting
You have recently formed a study group with several classmates. You are responsible for organizing the group's first meeting and want to make sure that the time, place, and preparation work for everyone.
Write an email to the members of the study group. In your email, do the following:
* Suggest a time and place for the first meeting.
* Explain what the group should accomplish during the meeting.
* Ask the members to share their availability and prepare something in advance.
Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

## My Polished Response
Hi everyone,
I am glad that we have formed a study group for the biology midterm. For our first meeting, I suggest meeting this Sunday from 2:00 to 4:00 p.m. in Study Room 3B at the main library. The room has a whiteboard and enough space for all six members.
During the meeting, we should first compare the professor's review guide with our class notes. Then we can divide the five major units among group members and decide how to share summaries and practice questions. I also hope we can set a regular weekly meeting time before we leave.
Please reply by Friday with your availability and mention any schedule conflicts. Before Sunday, could each person bring one difficult concept and two possible exam questions? This preparation will help us use the meeting efficiently.
I look forward to studying together.
Best,
Alex Lee
