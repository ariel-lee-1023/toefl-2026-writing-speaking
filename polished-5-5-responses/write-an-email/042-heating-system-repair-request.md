# Heating System Repair Request

## Prompt
You have recently moved into off-campus housing and are experiencing issues with the heating system. You need to contact the property manager, Ms. Green, to report the problem and request a repair.

Write an email to Ms. Green. In your email, do the following.

- Describe the issues you are experiencing with the heating system.
- Explain how these issues are affecting your daily life.
- Request a repair and ask for a timeline for when it will be fixed.

Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

To: Ms. Green
Subject: Heating system repair request

## My Polished Response
Dear Ms. Green,

I am reaching out because the heating system in my new off-campus housing is not working properly. It fails to warm my room and makes loud rattling noises whenever it is running.

The room remains uncomfortably cold, especially at night, and the noise makes it difficult to sleep. I am preparing for my final exam, so the cold and lack of rest are also making it harder to concentrate on studying.

Could you please arrange for a technician to inspect and repair the heating system as soon as possible? I would appreciate it if you could let me know when someone can visit and when you expect the repairs to be completed.

Thank you for your help.

Sincerely,
Ariel
