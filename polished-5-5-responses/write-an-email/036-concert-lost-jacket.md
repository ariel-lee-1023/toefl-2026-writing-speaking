# Concert Lost Jacket

## Prompt
Background:
You recently attended your university's choir concert and had a great experience. However, you accidentally left your favorite jacket behind. You need to contact the university's lost and found department.
Requirements:
1. Explain when and where you left your jacket
2. Describe the jacket in detail
3. Suggest a solution for how the lost item could be returned to you

## My Polished Response
Dear Lost and Found Staff,

I am writing because I believe I accidentally left my jacket at the university choir concert last Friday evening. I was sitting in the third row of the main auditorium, and I may have placed it on the back of my chair before leaving. The jacket is white and medium-sized, featuring a small silver zipper on the left pocket.

Additionally, there is a student ID card with my name on it located in one of the inside pockets. I am very anxious to recover it because the jacket was a special gift from my parents, and I rely on it during sunny days to protect against direct sunlight. If someone has turned it in, could you please keep it at your office?

I would be more than happy to stop by and pick it up this afternoon or at another time that is convenient for your staff.

Best regards,
Ariel Lee
