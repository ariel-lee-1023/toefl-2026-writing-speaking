# Pet Adoption Advice

## Prompt
Your friend Alex is considering adopting a dog and has asked for your advice. She often feels lonely and hopes to get a dog as a companion, but she lives in a small apartment and does not have much space.
Write an email to Alex. In your email, do the following.

Describe the advantages and disadvantages of adopting a dog.
Give Alex your advice and explain why.
Suggest other ways to help with her loneliness.
Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

## My Polished Response
Dear Alex, I am sorry to hear that you have been feeling lonely lately, but I fully understand why you are looking for a companion to help you feel less isolated. Adopting a dog can certainly bring warmth and joy to a home, and it naturally encourages you to get outside for daily exercise. However, caring for a dog requires significant living space, constant attention, and a predictable schedule. Given your long work hours and compact apartment, taking on a dog right now might create more stress than comfort. I strongly advise you to hold off on dog ownership for now and consider adopting an older cat instead, as cats adapt far better to smaller spaces and do not mind being alone while you are at work. Beyond getting a pet, there are several other ways we can tackle your loneliness together. First, we should make a point to hang out more frequently next semester, whether that means studying together or getting coffee on weekends. Second, you might join a campus interest club to meet people who share your hobbies. Finally, volunteering at a local animal shelter would let you spend time with dogs without the full-time commitment. Best, Ariel Lee

## My Draft
Dear Alex, I am sorry for your loneliness and fully understand you need to feel less isolated. Adopting a dog can enhance a family's warmth and kindness, and it can lead you to take outdoor walks every day. But each dog needs space to live and regular companion, and your work is quite intense. Your apartment is also too small for a dog. I think you should wait and maybe adopt a cat instead, because cats need less space. Also, we should talk more, hang out next semester, and you could join a club or volunteer at an animal shelter. Sincerely, Arial Lee

## My Diagnosis
The original draft earns a Score 4 due to adequate but limited elaboration and minor phrasing inaccuracies. The cons of dog ownership were stated broadly ("your work is quite intense", "your apartment is also too small"), lacking concrete details about scheduling or spatial constraints. Additionally, expressions like "regular companion" and "enhance a family's warmth and kindness" were slightly unidiomatic, and the suggestions for handling loneliness were lumped together without connective scaffolding.

## My What Changed & Why
1. Expanded Elaboration: Expanded general claims into concrete, realistic details—such as explaining that an older cat minds being alone less during long work hours, and specifying coffee/study dates for social connection. This meets the Score 5 requirement for fully elaborated and relevant support.
2. Refined Precision & Register: Fixed unidiomatic phrasing by changing "regular companion" to "constant attention" and replacing abrupt claims like "your apartment is also too small" with qualified statements ("Given your long work hours and compact apartment...").
3. Enhanced Flow and Scaffolding: Grouped the three prompt tasks into clear paragraph transitions. Applied explicit sequencing markers (First, Second, Finally) to structure the alternative suggestions in continuous prose without relying on bullet points.
