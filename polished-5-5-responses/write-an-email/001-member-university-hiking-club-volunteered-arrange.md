# Member University Hiking Club Volunteered Arrange

## Prompt
You are a member of your university’s hiking club and have volunteered to arrange hotel accommodations for the club’s upcoming trip to Green Mountain. There are fifteen to twenty people in your group, and you will need to check out at 4 a.m. to start hiking. Another member has recommended Green Mountain Inn, where he has stayed before.
Write an email to Lisa Ngo, manager at Green Mountain Inn. In your email, do the following.
Explain how you heard about the inn.
Describe your schedule and needs.
Ask for availability and room rates.
Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

## My Polished Response
To: Lisa Ngo
Subject: Group lodging inquiry for university hiking club (15–20 guests)

Dear Ms. Ngo,

I am writing on behalf of our university hiking club because a member who stayed at Green Mountain Inn last spring recommended it highly and mentioned how smoothly your staff handled an early departure. We are planning an overnight stay for fifteen to twenty people the night before we begin our Green Mountain trek, and we will need to leave the property by 4:00 a.m. so that we can reach the trailhead at first light.

Given that schedule, I would be grateful if you could confirm whether an early check-out of that kind can be arranged without difficulty, and whether you have any double or triple rooms available that weekend. Could you also let me know the nightly rates for a group of this size and whether any group discount applies?

Sincerely,
Alex Reed
