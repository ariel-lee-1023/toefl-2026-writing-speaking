# Requesting Recommendation Letter

## Prompt
You are applying to a graduate program in education and need a letter of recommendation from a former instructor. Professor Kim taught your favorite undergraduate course two years ago, and you earned a high grade in her class. You would like to ask her to write a letter on your behalf.
Write an email to Professor Kim. In your email, do the following:

Remind her of the course you took together.
Explain why you are applying to the program.
Provide the deadline for submitting the letter.
Write as much as you can and in complete sentences.

## My Polished Response
Dear Professor Kim,

I hope this email finds you well. I am writing to ask if you would be willing to write a letter of recommendation for my application to the Master’s program in Cultural Studies at State University.

I thoroughly enjoyed taking your "Media and Society" course two years ago. Earning an A in your class and receiving your insightful feedback deeply influenced my decision to pursue advanced research in this field. Since graduating, my passion for analyzing modern media systems has only grown, and I feel well-prepared to take this next step in my academic journey.

The deadline for submitting the recommendation letter is December 15. If you are able to write the letter, I would be happy to send over my updated CV, my statement of purpose, and my unofficial transcripts to assist you. 

Thank you very much for your time, support, and consideration.

Sincerely,  
Ariel Lee
