# Damaged Furniture Delivery

## Prompt
Damaged Furniture
You recently ordered a piece of furniture for your apartment. When it arrived, you discovered that it had been damaged during delivery. You need to contact the company's customer-service department and request a solution.
Write an email to customer service. In your email, do the following:
* Describe the furniture and the damage you found.
* Explain how the problem has affected you.
* Request a specific solution to the problem.

## My Polished Response
Dear Customer Service,
I am writing about the wooden desk I ordered from your website last week. It arrived yesterday, but one corner of the desktop is badly cracked and two of the metal legs are bent. The shipping box was also torn on one side, so the damage may have occurred during delivery.
I purchased the desk because I need a stable place to study for my final exams. At the moment, it cannot stand safely, and I have had to continue working at a small kitchen table. I have attached photographs of the damaged parts and the packaging.
Could you please send a replacement desk at no additional cost and arrange to collect the damaged one? If a replacement is unavailable this week, I would prefer a full refund.
Please let me know the next steps and the expected delivery date.
Best,
Alex Lee
