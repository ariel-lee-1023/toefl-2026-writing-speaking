# 🧠 Active Cognitive Buffer: The University Dilemma

## 1. Prefrontal Abstraction (The Core Thesis)
* **Core Thesis:** Although modern elite universities are trapped in an unsustainable cycle of escalating costs and rigid traditional models, they must undergo a paradigm shift toward flexible, AI-integrated, and learner-centric designs to survive.

## 2. Associative Evidence Mapping (Logical Architecture)
* **Pillar A (Context/Problem):** Driven by an arms race for reputation and prestige, traditional universities have over-invested in sustaining innovations, leading to soaring operational costs that no longer align with the practical skill demands of modern learners.
* **Pillar B (Mechanism/Intervention):** By applying design thinking to fundamentally reimagine the educational experience from the ground up, institutions can transition from monolithic degree programs to flexible, capability-focused frameworks that treat AI as a core cognitive exoskeleton.
* **Pillar C (Implication/Result):** This paradigm shift will ultimately replace the standardized industrial education model with a lifelong, highly personalized learning ecosystem that prioritizes human-AI collaboration and self-driven adaptability.
