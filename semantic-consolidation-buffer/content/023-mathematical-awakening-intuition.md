# 🧠 Active Cognitive Buffer: Mathematical Awakening And Intuition

## 1. Prefrontal Abstraction (The Core Thesis)
* **Core Thesis:** Although many people mistakenly believe that advanced mathematical ability relies exclusively on rigorous logical deduction and inherent genius, true mathematical proficiency actually stems from cultivating visual intuition to translate abstract formulas back into natural mental images.

## 2. Associative Evidence Mapping (Logical Architecture)
* **Pillar A (Context/Problem):** Traditional education heavily emphasizes complex formulas and tedious logical steps, which causes learners to lose the underlying visual intuition and feel deeply frustrated by seemingly magical mathematical tricks.
* **Pillar B (Mechanism/Intervention):** By actively translating abstract symbols back into concrete mental images and utilizing logic strictly to test and refine these intuitive models rather than to generate them, learners can develop a profound comprehension of mathematical concepts.
* **Pillar C (Implication/Result):** This intuitive approach ultimately allows individuals to grasp mathematical proofs as natural and inevitable, enabling them to tackle complex original research with confidence and independent insight.
