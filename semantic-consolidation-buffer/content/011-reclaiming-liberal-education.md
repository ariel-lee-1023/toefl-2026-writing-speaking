# 🧠 Active Cognitive Buffer: Reclaiming Liberal Education

## 1. Prefrontal Abstraction (The Core Thesis)
* **Core Thesis:** Although a liberal education was originally designed to cultivate the civic responsibilities and intellectual humility required for a functioning free society, American universities have increasingly abandoned this mission in favor of ideological indoctrination, necessitating a multi-front reform effort to restore viewpoint diversity and rigorous academic inquiry.

## 2. Associative Evidence Mapping (Logical Architecture)
* **Pillar A (Context/Problem):** Over the past several decades, the academic establishment has been gradually overtaken by a blend of postmodern relativism and radical political activism -> resulting in a self-replicating faculty culture that suppresses dissenting viewpoints -> and prioritizes the transmission of specific political orthodoxies over the objective exploration of historical truths.
* **Pillar B (Mechanism/Intervention):** This ideological capture has manifested in the implementation of rigid speech codes, the penalization of empirical analysis that challenges preferred narratives, and the reliance on peer-review systems that act as ideological gatekeepers -> which collectively destroy the foundational Socratic dialogue championed by classical liberal thinkers.
* **Pillar C (Implication/Result):** To counter this deep-seated institutional decay, reform cannot rely solely on the actions of individual university presidents -> but must instead involve a comprehensive strategy that includes establishing new civic thought programs and creating alternative educational institutions -> aiming to systematically rebuild a robust academic environment.
