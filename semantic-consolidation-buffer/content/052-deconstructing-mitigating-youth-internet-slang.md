# 🧠 Active Cognitive Buffer: Deconstructing and Mitigating Youth Internet Slang

## 1. Prefrontal Abstraction (The Core Thesis)
* **Core Thesis:** Although the proliferation of low-effort internet slang among youth serves as a low-barrier mechanism for peer group identification, it fundamentally degrades critical cognitive expression by replacing nuanced discourse with conversational termination tropes.

## 2. Associative Evidence Mapping (Logical Architecture)
* **Pillar A (Context/Problem):** Modern digital algorithms and the blurring of adult-child cultural boundaries have accelerated the spread of vulgar meme language, leaving adolescents in a state of cognitive decay where meaningful internal feelings cannot be articulated.
* **Pillar B (Mechanism/Intervention):** Direct prohibition by authority figures counterproductively triggers rebellious impulses and collective repetition, whereas systematic pedagogical dilution gradually displaces empty buzzwords by rewarding authentic output.
* **Pillar C (Implication/Result):** Shifting educational emphasis from restricting input to scaffolding meaningful L2-register expression restores underlying analytical capacity and prevents conversational premature termination.
