# 🧠 Active Cognitive Buffer: Technology And Human Abundance

## 1. Prefrontal Abstraction (The Core Thesis)
* **Core Thesis:** While humanity has achieved god-like technological power that creates an era of abundance, individuals must adopt exponential mindsets and avoid outsourcing their core cognition to AI in order to meaningfully harness this power.

## 2. Associative Evidence Mapping (Logical Architecture)
* **Pillar A (Context/Problem):** Rapid, exponential technological growth collides with our linear evolutionary brains and slow institutional adaptations, causing people to feel anxious and threatened by job displacement rather than experiencing the actual abundance.
* **Pillar B (Mechanism/Intervention):** Adapting to this era requires shifting from a scarcity mindset to "moonshot" and exponential thinking, where individuals set audacious goals within theoretical limits and retain their independent analytical framing before consulting AI.
* **Pillar C (Implication/Result):** As AI makes basic execution and answers cheaper, human value transitions toward asking superior questions, maintaining personal taste, and solving unique problems that depend on individualized empathy and context.
