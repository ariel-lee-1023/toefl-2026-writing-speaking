# 🧠 Active Cognitive Buffer: The Truth About Cancer

## 1. Prefrontal Abstraction (The Core Thesis)
* **Core Thesis:** Although cancer is widely feared as an insurmountable and homogenous disease, it is actually a highly complex group of endogenous cellular mutations that can be systematically managed through rigorous preventative screening, lifestyle modifications, and advanced targeted therapies.

## 2. Associative Evidence Mapping (Logical Architecture)
* **Pillar A (Context/Problem):** Because malignant tumors originate from the body's own mutating cells and constantly develop drug resistance, traditional broad-spectrum treatments like chemotherapy often result in severe collateral damage to healthy tissues.
* **Pillar B (Mechanism/Intervention):** By identifying specific genetic mutations and leveraging the body's natural defense mechanisms, modern medicine has developed targeted therapies and immunotherapies to attack cancer cells with high precision.
* **Pillar C (Implication/Result):** Consequently, integrating these advanced combinatorial treatments with widespread early screening and the active avoidance of known carcinogens significantly improves patient survival rates and overall prognosis.
