# 🧠 Active Cognitive Buffer: Centralization and Decentralization in Pain and Psychology

## 1. Prefrontal Abstraction (The Core Thesis)
* **Core Thesis:** While individuals naturally seek immediate relief by avoiding physical or emotional discomfort, true recovery requires systematic, incremental exposure toward the central source of stress, because avoidance triggers decentralization that diffuses anxiety into broader environmental triggers.

## 2. Associative Evidence Mapping (Logical Architecture)
* **Pillar A (Context/Problem):** Patients and individuals experiencing discomfort frequently misinterpret temporary symptom reduction as true recovery -> they adopt compensatory avoidance strategies -> the underlying mechanical or psychological strain expands outward into secondary domains.
* **Pillar B (Mechanism/Intervention):** Mechanical Diagnosis and Therapy demonstrates that shifts in pain location outweigh mere intensity changes -> targeted movement brings peripheral pain back toward the spine -> controlled exposure similarly condenses psychological avoidance into specific, manageable triggers.
* **Pillar C (Implication/Result):** Focusing on position centralization over immediate comfort prevents compensatory behavior -> structured incremental confrontation clarifies the exact stressor -> long-term mechanical or psychological resilience is re-established.
