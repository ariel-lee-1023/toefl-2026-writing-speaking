# 🧠 Active Cognitive Buffer: Physiological Impacts of Sedentary Behavior

## 1. Prefrontal Abstraction (The Core Thesis)
* **Core Thesis:** Although modern knowledge work and technological conveniences enforce prolonged sedentary behavior that severely disrupts metabolic and vascular functions, integrating frequent micro-movements throughout the day can actively reverse these physiological deficits and restore internal bodily awareness.

## 2. Associative Evidence Mapping (Logical Architecture)
* **Pillar A (Context/Problem):** Prolonged sitting kinks the vascular system and compresses the diaphragm while screens suppress the body's internal signaling, which -> starves muscles and the brain of oxygen and glucose -> and creates chronic fatigue, metabolic dysfunction, and severe myopia in both adults and children.
* **Pillar B (Mechanism/Intervention):** Implementing a regimen of merely five minutes of gentle walking for every thirty minutes of sitting acts as a mechanical reset, which -> dramatically lowers blood glucose and blood pressure -> and simultaneously improves cognitive focus, mood, and overall productivity by breaking the illusion that stationary grinding equates to effective work.
* **Pillar C (Implication/Result):** Because intense, concentrated workouts cannot undo the cumulative damage of remaining an "active couch potato" for the rest of the day, individuals and organizations must enact a systemic cultural shift, which -> reintroduces normalized micro-movements into school and office environments -> and embraces a balanced technological engagement rather than demanding unrealistic digital abstinence.
