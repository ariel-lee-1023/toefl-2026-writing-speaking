# 🧠 Active Cognitive Buffer: Cognitive Manipulation And Propaganda

## 1. Prefrontal Abstraction (The Core Thesis)
* **Core Thesis:** Although individuals generally believe they make decisions based on logical reasoning and objective facts, propagandists routinely manipulate mass behavior by exploiting cognitive biases and triggering heuristic, fast-thinking pathways through emotional arousal and selective framing.

## 2. Associative Evidence Mapping (Logical Architecture)
* **Pillar A (Context/Problem):** Because human evolution favors energy-efficient cognitive shortcuts, individuals are highly susceptible to peripheral persuasion cues such as pre-framing, perceived source credibility, and intense emotional appeals.
* **Pillar B (Mechanism/Intervention):** Manipulators exploit deeply ingrained psychological mechanisms, like cognitive dissonance and the bandwagon effect, to force audiences to rationalize blatantly false narratives rather than confronting uncomfortable truths.
* **Pillar C (Implication/Result):** Consequently, to protect their autonomy and make rational choices, individuals must actively engage their slow-thinking analytical faculties to scrutinize information sources, reject premature definitions, and systematically interrogate opposing arguments.
