# 🧠 Active Cognitive Buffer: Residual Judgment Rights and Organizational Authority

## 1. Prefrontal Abstraction (The Core Thesis)
* **Core Thesis:** While market mechanisms, rule-based systems, and democratic voting handle standardized operations, firm survival requires a centralized authority to hold residual judgment rights when allocating resources under unquantifiable environmental uncertainty.

## 2. Associative Evidence Mapping (Logical Architecture)
* **Pillar A (Context/Problem):** Market transactions incur search and negotiation costs, while complete contracts cannot anticipate every future contingency -> organizations cannot resolve complex strategic trade-offs through voting without generating contradictory preferences -> operational paralysis threatens firm stability during novel crises.
* **Pillar B (Mechanism/Intervention):** Employment agreements transfer discretion over unspecified scenarios to institutional leadership -> executive authority reserves residual judgment specifically for high-coupling, irreversible strategic choices -> routine, reversible decisions are delegated to frontline specialists.
* **Pillar C (Implication/Result):** Organizational friction decreases as decision rights align with situational risk -> ambiguous strategic options are compressed into a unified institutional commitment -> the enterprise maintains coherence and adaptation under Knightian uncertainty.
