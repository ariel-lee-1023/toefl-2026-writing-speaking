# 🧠 Active Cognitive Buffer: Modern Life Psychological Epidemics

## 1. Prefrontal Abstraction (The Core Thesis)
* **Core Thesis:** While modern digital conveniences promise unprecedented ease and connection, and even though individuals increasingly optimize measurable proxies like wealth and follower counts to achieve fulfillment, these false metrics actively strip away intrinsic satisfaction, resulting in widespread psychological epidemics of restlessness, inadequacy, isolation, and existential invisibility.

## 2. Associative Evidence Mapping (Logical Architecture)
* **Pillar A (Context/Problem):** Constant exposure to infinite digital feeds and algorithmic social media traps individuals in unwinnable comparative loops -> forces them to measure their self-worth against unrealistic, curated standards -> and cultivates chronic feelings of falling behind and profound interpersonal isolation despite physical proximity.
* **Pillar B (Mechanism/Intervention):** Because humans mistakenly apply Goodhart's Law to their personal lives by treating easily measurable proxies as primary objectives -> they lose sight of their underlying psychological needs for safety, belonging, and significance -> leading to a restless cycle of achieving goals without ever feeling fulfilled.
* **Pillar C (Implication/Result):** By deliberately identifying the true emotional void behind each symptom and directly pursuing the underlying need -> individuals can bypass algorithmic stunt doubles -> and anchor their psychological well-being in authentic, unquantifiable human experiences, such as competing only with one's past self.
