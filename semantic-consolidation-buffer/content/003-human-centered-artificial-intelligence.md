# 🧠 Active Cognitive Buffer: Human Centered Artificial Intelligence

## 1. Prefrontal Abstraction (The Core Thesis)
* **Core Thesis:** Although artificial intelligence has achieved unprecedented capabilities through data-driven neural networks and expanded into spatial intelligence, and even though these technologies can significantly augment human problem-solving in fields like medicine and education, AI fundamentally lacks the unquantifiable emotional intuition of human cognition, necessitating a collaborative, human-centered governance framework that prioritizes human agency.

## 2. Associative Evidence Mapping (Logical Architecture)
* **Pillar A (Context/Problem):** The convergence of massive internet data sets and advanced GPU compute capabilities enabled neural networks to mimic basic biological visual processing, which subsequently drove the leap from simple object recognition to complex video generation based entirely on statistical probability rather than genuine comprehension.
* **Pillar B (Mechanism/Intervention):** Because current machine learning models rely exclusively on digitized, uploaded human behavior, they fundamentally lack access to unrecorded, highly individualized human experiences, which prevents AI from replicating genuine emotional empathy or spontaneous human intuition.
* **Pillar C (Implication/Result):** Recognizing AI as a cognitive enhancer rather than a human replacement shifts the societal focus toward multi-stakeholder governance and specialized applications—such as robotic surgical assistance and personalized educational prompting—which ultimately preserves human agency while radically accelerating scientific discovery.
