# 🧠 Active Cognitive Buffer: US Higher Education Wokeism

## 1. Prefrontal Abstraction (The Core Thesis)
* **Core Thesis:** Although diversity initiatives in American elite higher education are publicly framed as moral progress, and even though social media has turbocharged this ideological shift, they actually function as a reputational defense mechanism that obscures the underlying crisis of predatory corporate models and the casualization of academic labor.

## 2. Associative Evidence Mapping (Logical Architecture)
* **Pillar A (Context/Problem):** Following the 2008 financial crisis and rising student debt, elite universities faced severe populist backlash, prompting administrators to distract the public from exorbitant tuition costs and predatory economic models by weaponizing performative social justice initiatives as a cheap moral shield.
* **Pillar B (Mechanism/Intervention):** To maintain this progressive facade, university administrations and external philanthropic foundations implemented top-down ideological mandates—such as cluster hiring and mandatory racism self-audits—forcing academics to adopt rigid social justice paradigms out of fear of institutional retaliation and online mobs.
* **Pillar C (Implication/Result):** Consequently, the traditional secure academic system has collapsed into a precarious gig-economy model for professors, leading to a profound erosion of intellectual diversity, the infantilization of students, and a catastrophic decline in public trust across the political spectrum.
