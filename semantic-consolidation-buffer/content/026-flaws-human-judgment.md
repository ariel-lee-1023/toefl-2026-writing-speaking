# 🧠 Active Cognitive Buffer: The Flaws Of Human Judgment

## 1. Prefrontal Abstraction (The Core Thesis)
* **Core Thesis:** Although human judgment is widely presumed to be affected primarily by predictable systemic biases, it is equally compromised by unpredictable, scattered variability known as noise, which must be systematically mitigated through structured decision-making processes.

## 2. Associative Evidence Mapping (Logical Architecture)
* **Pillar A (Context/Problem):** Because human judgment inherently contains both predictable cognitive biases and unpredictable variations known as noise, critical decisions in fields like justice and medicine are often plagued by severe and unnoticed inconsistencies.
* **Pillar B (Mechanism/Intervention):** By cultivating actively open-minded thinking and implementing structured mediating assessments—where complex decisions are broken down into independent, externally evaluated dimensions—organizations can significantly reduce situational and systemic noise.
* **Pillar C (Implication/Result):** Consequently, rather than relying on premature intuition, decision-makers who utilize these structured processes can achieve much higher accuracy, fairness, and overall decision quality.
