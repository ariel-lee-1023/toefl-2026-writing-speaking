# 🧠 Active Cognitive Buffer: Structural Distortion in AI Automated Talent Acquisition

## 1. Prefrontal Abstraction (The Core Thesis)
* **Core Thesis:** Even though automated resume optimization tools enable job seekers to submit tailored applications at scale, this reciprocal reliance on artificial intelligence in hiring creates an escalating screening arms race that homogenizes candidate profiles and obscures genuine vocational commitment.

## 2. Associative Evidence Mapping (Logical Architecture)
* **Pillar A (Context/Problem):** Job seekers utilize automated tools to generate vast quantities of tailored resumes -> corporate recruiters experience exponential application volume increases -> reduced HR headcount necessitates defensive automated algorithmic filtering.
* **Pillar B (Mechanism/Intervention):** Dual-sided algorithmic optimization replaces genuine human evaluation -> applicant materials become standardized to pass keyword filters -> recruiters gain no deeper qualitative understanding of individual candidates despite processing thousands of entries.
* **Pillar C (Implication/Result):** Traditional credentials lose signaling value under heavy AI usage -> post-offer revocations and interview friction escalate -> proactive problem-solving initiative and intrinsic motivation emerge as the primary market differentiators.
