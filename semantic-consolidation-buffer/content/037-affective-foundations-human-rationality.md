# 🧠 Active Cognitive Buffer: Affective Foundations of Human Rationality

## 1. Prefrontal Abstraction (The Core Thesis)
* **Core Thesis:** Although subjective emotional expressions are often perceived as irrational impulses that hinder logical processing, emotional states operate as a foundational cognitive driver that optimizes decision-making, activates adaptive behavior, and reinforces moral cohesion.

## 2. Associative Evidence Mapping (Logical Architecture)
* **Pillar A (Context/Problem):** Traditional cognitive paradigms mischaracterize affective states as disruptive noise that destabilizes rational decision-making -> suppressing emotional feedback compromises risk evaluation and eliminates basic self-preservation mechanisms -> individuals without functional affective processing fail to execute adaptive choices despite intact logical reasoning.
* **Pillar B (Mechanism/Intervention):** Primary emotional responses function as evolutionary signaling systems that process environmental threats and opportunities across cognitive stages -> targeted interventions (such as mindfulness meditation for cortisol regulation, deliberate cognitive framing during anger, and written processing for rumination) recalibrate hyperactive affective alarms -> emotional activation aligns with objective situational demands rather than escalating into dysfunction.
* **Pillar C (Implication/Result):** Re-integrating regulated emotional responses into conscious reasoning restores basic risk aversion, prosocial empathy, and psychological recovery mechanisms -> affective processing serves as the operational engine for rational goal pursuit -> individuals navigate complex social environments with heightened resilience and moral integrity.
