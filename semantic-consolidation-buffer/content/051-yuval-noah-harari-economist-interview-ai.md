# 🧠 Active Cognitive Buffer: Yuval Noah Harari Economist Interview on AI Risks

## 1. Prefrontal Abstraction (The Core Thesis)
* **Core Thesis:** While current AI acceleration makes superintelligence and autonomous systemic control highly likely within a decade, this trajectory remains non-inevitable and can be redirected if human societies actively restrict AI personhood and enforce transparent human-AI boundaries before critical agency thresholds are crossed.

## 2. Associative Evidence Mapping (Logical Architecture)
* **Pillar A (Context/Problem):** The rapid migration of human trust from traditional institutions to opaque algorithmic systems has created a severe responsibility vacuum, where market forces prioritize performance over safety while industry leaders exploit narratives of inevitability to evade regulatory accountability.
* **Pillar B (Mechanism/Intervention):** Unchecked technical progression enables AI to mass-produce synthetic intimacy and autonomous financial entities, which systematically lowers the barrier for political manipulation and risks completely locking humans out of decision-making loops across economic and military domains.
* **Pillar C (Implication/Result):** Proactive regulatory enforcement that strictly prohibits AI legal personhood, outlaws synthetic human impersonation, and enforces human-in-the-loop operational constraints remains the sole viable pathway to safeguard democratic agency and prevent catastrophic institutional capture.
