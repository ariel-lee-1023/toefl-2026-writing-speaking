# 🧠 Active Cognitive Buffer: The Structure Of Scientific Revolutions

## 1. Prefrontal Abstraction (The Core Thesis)
* **Core Thesis:** Although scientific progress is traditionally viewed as a continuous, linear accumulation of objective facts, true scientific advancement actually occurs through discontinuous, revolutionary paradigm shifts triggered by the undeniable accumulation of theoretical anomalies.

## 2. Associative Evidence Mapping (Logical Architecture)
* **Pillar A (Context/Problem):** Once a mature scientific discipline establishes a dominant paradigm, researchers engage in routine puzzle-solving until they inevitably encounter persistent anomalies that the existing theoretical framework cannot explain.
* **Pillar B (Mechanism/Intervention):** As these anomalies accumulate and plunge the scientific community into crisis, scientists propose radically new frameworks, leading to a sudden, non-rational shift in the community's collective perspective.
* **Pillar C (Implication/Result):** This paradigm shift fundamentally redefines the discipline's conceptual boundaries, ultimately driving scientific progress by expanding the explanatory power and predictive accuracy of the new models.
