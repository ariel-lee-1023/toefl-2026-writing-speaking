# 🧠 Active Cognitive Buffer: The Tech Rebel Framework for Adolescent Digital Autonomy

## 1. Prefrontal Abstraction (The Core Thesis)
* **Core Thesis:** Although modern smartphone applications exploit variable-ratio reinforcement schedules to capture youth attention, adolescents can successfully reclaim cognitive autonomy by internalizing a rebellious self-identity against algorithmic manipulation.

## 2. Associative Evidence Mapping (Logical Architecture)
* **Pillar A (Context/Problem):** Digital platforms intentionally leverage random reward structures and social validation cues to minimize usage friction, reducing adolescent self-efficacy and inducing behavioral dependency.
* **Pillar B (Mechanism/Intervention):** Introducing operational friction, such as greyscale display modes and peer-supported accountability partnerships, interrupts automated physical checking habits and exposes underlying algorithmic design tricks.
* **Pillar C (Implication/Result):** Replacing screen consumption with high-engagement, embodiment-driven real-world experiences permanently restructures adolescent self-conception from passive system-user to autonomous agency holder.
