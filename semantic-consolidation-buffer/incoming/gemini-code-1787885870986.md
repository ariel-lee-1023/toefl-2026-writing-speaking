## Title
Deconstructing and Mitigating Youth Internet Slang

## TOEFL Domain
Linguistics

## Tier
T2 (D≈5)

## Core Thesis
Although the proliferation of low-effort internet slang among youth serves as a low-barrier mechanism for peer group identification, it fundamentally degrades critical cognitive expression by replacing nuanced discourse with conversational termination tropes.

## Pillar A
Modern digital algorithms and the blurring of adult-child cultural boundaries have accelerated the spread of vulgar meme language, leaving adolescents in a state of cognitive decay where meaningful internal feelings cannot be articulated.

## Pillar B
Direct prohibition by authority figures counterproductively triggers rebellious impulses and collective repetition, whereas systematic pedagogical dilution gradually displaces empty buzzwords by rewarding authentic output.

## Pillar C
Shifting educational emphasis from restricting input to scaffolding meaningful L2-register expression restores underlying analytical capacity and prevents conversational premature termination.
