## Title
Technology And Human Abundance

## TOEFL Domain
Technology and Society

## Tier
T2 (D≈6.0)

## Core Thesis
While humanity has achieved god-like technological power that creates an era of abundance, individuals must adopt exponential mindsets and avoid outsourcing their core cognition to AI in order to meaningfully harness this power.

## Pillar A
Rapid, exponential technological growth collides with our linear evolutionary brains and slow institutional adaptations, causing people to feel anxious and threatened by job displacement rather than experiencing the actual abundance[cite: 15].

## Pillar B
Adapting to this era requires shifting from a scarcity mindset to "moonshot" and exponential thinking, where individuals set audacious goals within theoretical limits and retain their independent analytical framing before consulting AI[cite: 15].

## Pillar C
As AI makes basic execution and answers cheaper, human value transitions toward asking superior questions, maintaining personal taste, and solving unique problems that depend on individualized empathy and context[cite: 15].
