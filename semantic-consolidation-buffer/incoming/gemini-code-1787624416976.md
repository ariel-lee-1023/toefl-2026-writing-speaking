## Title
The Structure Of Scientific Revolutions

## TOEFL Domain
History of Science

## Tier
T2 (D≈6.3)

## Core Thesis
Although scientific progress is traditionally viewed as a continuous, linear accumulation of objective facts, true scientific advancement actually occurs through discontinuous, revolutionary paradigm shifts triggered by the undeniable accumulation of theoretical anomalies.

## Pillar A
Once a mature scientific discipline establishes a dominant paradigm, researchers engage in routine puzzle-solving until they inevitably encounter persistent anomalies that the existing theoretical framework cannot explain.

## Pillar B
As these anomalies accumulate and plunge the scientific community into crisis, scientists propose radically new frameworks, leading to a sudden, non-rational shift in the community's collective perspective.

## Pillar C
This paradigm shift fundamentally redefines the discipline's conceptual boundaries, ultimately driving scientific progress by expanding the explanatory power and predictive accuracy of the new models.
