## Title
The End Of Work Society

## TOEFL Domain
Sociology

## Tier
T2 (D≈6.6)

## Core Thesis
Although modern civilization has elevated paid employment to the central axis of human identity and social stability, the exponential rise of automation and globalization is dismantling this structure, necessitating a radical redefinition of work and societal value.

## Pillar A
Because the traditional work-centric society relies on full employment to sustain welfare and individual identity, the rapid elimination of jobs by advanced algorithms and globalized capital flows is creating widespread precariousness and social alienation[cite: 18].

## Pillar B
To counter this systemic collapse, society must implement new frameworks such as universal basic income, recognize unpaid civic and caregiving labor, and legitimize diverse, flexible forms of employment[cite: 18].

## Pillar C
Ultimately, decoupling human worth from traditional paid labor will allow individuals to pursue deeper existential meaning through creativity, community building, and self-directed civic participation[cite: 18].
