## Title
The Tech Rebel Framework for Adolescent Digital Autonomy

## TOEFL Domain
Psychology

## Tier
T2 (D≈5)

## Core Thesis
Although modern smartphone applications exploit variable-ratio reinforcement schedules to capture youth attention, adolescents can successfully reclaim cognitive autonomy by internalizing a rebellious self-identity against algorithmic manipulation.

## Pillar A
Digital platforms intentionally leverage random reward structures and social validation cues to minimize usage friction, reducing adolescent self-efficacy and inducing behavioral dependency.

## Pillar B
Introducing operational friction, such as greyscale display modes and peer-supported accountability partnerships, interrupts automated physical checking habits and exposes underlying algorithmic design tricks.

## Pillar C
Replacing screen consumption with high-engagement, embodiment-driven real-world experiences permanently restructures adolescent self-conception from passive system-user to autonomous agency holder.
