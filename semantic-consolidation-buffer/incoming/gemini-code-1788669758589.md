## Title
AI and Programming Education

## TOEFL Domain
Education

## Tier
T2 (D≈5)

## Core Thesis
Although advanced language models can now effortlessly generate complex computer code, universities must still mandate traditional programming courses for science and engineering students, because the cognitive struggle inherent in writing code manually is essential for developing true analytical reasoning and evidence-based judgment.

## Pillar A
The rapid integration of artificial intelligence tools into academic and professional settings creates a false sense of sudden insight, leading individuals to conflate mere exposure to clear information with genuine cognitive mastery and long-term memory formation.

## Pillar B
Mandating hands-on programming forces students to personally navigate the intellectual friction of problem-solving, which causes the structural neural shifts required to transition from confusion to actual comprehension.

## Pillar C
By cultivating this firsthand operational experience, students build the critical evaluation skills necessary to verify AI-generated outputs, preventing catastrophic errors in high-stakes professional environments where unverified hallucinations can lead to severe consequences.
