## Title
AI Workforce Transition Policy

## TOEFL Domain
Economics

## Tier
T3 (D≈19)

## Core Thesis
Although artificial intelligence threatens to displace millions of workers in the short term, and even though past technological transitions like the "China Shock" led to severe economic instability due to reactive bureaucratic interventions, proactive collaboration between private sector tech leaders and government entities can establish robust retraining pipelines that safeguard national competitiveness.

## Pillar A
The rapid deployment of AI technologies threatens to eliminate entry-level and cognitive labor jobs faster than the economy can create new ones, which -> exacerbates existing labor-capital wealth gaps -> and threatens severe socioeconomic destabilization and extreme political polarization.

## Pillar B
By forming non-partisan coalitions like "Raise Us," policymakers and AI corporations can bypass rigid federal unemployment systems -> implement localized, AI-driven career navigation platforms and state-level apprenticeship programs -> and directly connect displaced workers with customized reskilling mapped to immediate employer demands.

## Pillar C
This proactive, market-aligned retraining approach mitigates the immediate trauma of job loss -> prevents the implementation of draconian, innovation-stifling AI regulations -> and ensures the United States maintains global technological leadership while preserving the economic security of its workforce.
