## Title
Paradigm Shifts in Computational Drug Discovery

## TOEFL Domain
Biology

## Tier
T3 (D≈15)

## Core Thesis
While traditional pharmaceutical discovery is severely constrained by high capital costs, prolonged development timelines, and low clinical success rates, computational AI modeling transforms target selection from passive screening to active molecular synthesis, thereby enabling viable personalized therapeutics.

## Pillar A
Conventional drug discovery relies on physical trial-and-error synthesis across vast chemical libraries -> astronomical financial expenditure and decade-long timelines occur -> over ninety percent of clinical candidates fail to reach commercial approval.

## Pillar B
Generative artificial intelligence models simulate protein structures and evaluate multi-million fragment libraries virtually -> algorithms modify molecular architectures at the atomic level -> optimized drug candidates are generated computationally in hours rather than years.

## Pillar C
In silico screening dramatically compresses pre-clinical timeline bottlenecks -> previously unapproachable or rare disease targets become economically viable -> pharmaceutical paradigms transition toward patient-specific therapeutic customization.
