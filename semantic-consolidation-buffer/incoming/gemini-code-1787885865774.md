## Title
Yuval Noah Harari Economist Interview on AI Risks

## TOEFL Domain
Computer Science

## Tier
T4 (D≈27)

## Core Thesis
While current AI acceleration makes superintelligence and autonomous systemic control highly likely within a decade, this trajectory remains non-inevitable and can be redirected if human societies actively restrict AI personhood and enforce transparent human-AI boundaries before critical agency thresholds are crossed.

## Pillar A
The rapid migration of human trust from traditional institutions to opaque algorithmic systems has created a severe responsibility vacuum, where market forces prioritize performance over safety while industry leaders exploit narratives of inevitability to evade regulatory accountability.

## Pillar B
Unchecked technical progression enables AI to mass-produce synthetic intimacy and autonomous financial entities, which systematically lowers the barrier for political manipulation and risks completely locking humans out of decision-making loops across economic and military domains.

## Pillar C
Proactive regulatory enforcement that strictly prohibits AI legal personhood, outlaws synthetic human impersonation, and enforces human-in-the-loop operational constraints remains the sole viable pathway to safeguard democratic agency and prevent catastrophic institutional capture.
