## Title
Cognitive Impact of Infinite Scroll

## TOEFL Domain
Cognitive Science

## Tier
T3 (D≈17)

## Core Thesis
While public discourse often relies on exaggerated myths about shrinking attention spans to condemn short-form media, and even though establishing definitive long-term causality remains challenging, empirical studies demonstrate that the autonomous, infinite-scroll interface of platforms like TikTok measurably degrades users' analytical thinking and prospective memory by stripping away their cognitive agency.

## Pillar A
Social media platforms have evolved into algorithmically optimized content dispensers -> this design deliberately removes user choice and relies on continuous, automated feeding -> fundamentally bypassing the user's conscious selection process and overriding intentional attention management.

## Pillar B
When users engage with these frictionless, infinite-scroll interfaces rather than structured, finite media formats -> their brains are lulled into a passive autopilot state -> which demonstrably impairs their ability to execute subsequent cognitive tasks requiring behavioral inhibition, analytical reasoning, and prospective memory.

## Pillar C
Acknowledging that the interface itself—rather than the length or topic of the content—is the primary driver of cognitive degradation allows policymakers and individuals to focus on restoring user agency -> pushing for platform architectures that reintroduce deliberate choices -> and safeguarding the human capacity for selective, intentional focus.
