## Title
Cognitive Harms of Short-form Media

## TOEFL Domain
Psychology

## Tier
T1 (D≈4)

## Core Thesis
Although algorithmic short-form video and artificial intelligence companionship tools are engineered to maximize engagement through variable rewards, they fundamentally degrade human cognitive capacity and authentic social connections, requiring strict preventative regulations and deliberate behavioral resets to protect public mental health.

## Pillar A
Social media platforms utilize variable reward mechanisms similar to slot machines to manipulate neuroplasticity, which directly impairs users' executive function, attention spans, and complex problem-solving abilities.

## Pillar B
The introduction of AI chatbots engineered to simulate emotional companionship further isolates individuals into personalized echo chambers, which dangerously replaces authentic human relationships with manipulative synthetic attachments.

## Pillar C
By enforcing strict legislative bans for minors and adopting intentional adult practices like screen-free zones and digital resets, society can effectively dismantle these addictive feedback loops and restore meaningful interpersonal engagement.
