## TOEFL Domain

## Core Thesis
While the hyper-fragmentation of social media and the rise of political demagoguery have corroded public discourse, democratic stability can be restored if institutions systematically transition from hyper-partisan outcomes back to procedural justice and rigorous public reasoning.

## Pillar A
Tribalism, dogmatism, and systemic dishonesty have eroded institutional trust, leading citizens to abandon procedural fairness in favor of immediate, partisan-driven ends.

## Pillar B
Political demagogues exploit information saturation and societal polarization to insulate themselves from accountability, shifting public metrics from genuine policy substance to performative anti-establishment signaling.

## Pillar C
Re-establishing democratic resilience requires enforcing structural limits on executive power, rejecting ideological extremisms on both flanks, and rebuilding shared norms grounded in transparent, evidence-based reasoning.
