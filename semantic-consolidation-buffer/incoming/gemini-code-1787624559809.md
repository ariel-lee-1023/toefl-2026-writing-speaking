## Title
The University Dilemma

## TOEFL Domain
Education

## Tier
T2 (D≈8.6)

## Core Thesis
Although modern elite universities are trapped in an unsustainable cycle of escalating costs and rigid traditional models, they must undergo a paradigm shift toward flexible, AI-integrated, and learner-centric designs to survive.

## Pillar A
Driven by an arms race for reputation and prestige, traditional universities have over-invested in sustaining innovations, leading to soaring operational costs that no longer align with the practical skill demands of modern learners[cite: 17].

## Pillar B
By applying design thinking to fundamentally reimagine the educational experience from the ground up, institutions can transition from monolithic degree programs to flexible, capability-focused frameworks that treat AI as a core cognitive exoskeleton[cite: 17].

## Pillar C
This paradigm shift will ultimately replace the standardized industrial education model with a lifelong, highly personalized learning ecosystem that prioritizes human-AI collaboration and self-driven adaptability[cite: 17].
