## Title
Human Centered Artificial Intelligence

## TOEFL Domain
Computer Science

## Tier
T4 (D≈26)

## Core Thesis
Although artificial intelligence has achieved unprecedented capabilities through data-driven neural networks and expanded into spatial intelligence[cite: 1], and even though these technologies can significantly augment human problem-solving in fields like medicine and education[cite: 1], AI fundamentally lacks the unquantifiable emotional intuition of human cognition, necessitating a collaborative, human-centered governance framework that prioritizes human agency[cite: 1].

## Pillar A
The convergence of massive internet data sets and advanced GPU compute capabilities enabled neural networks to mimic basic biological visual processing[cite: 1], which subsequently drove the leap from simple object recognition to complex video generation based entirely on statistical probability rather than genuine comprehension[cite: 1].

## Pillar B
Because current machine learning models rely exclusively on digitized, uploaded human behavior[cite: 1], they fundamentally lack access to unrecorded, highly individualized human experiences, which prevents AI from replicating genuine emotional empathy or spontaneous human intuition[cite: 1].

## Pillar C
Recognizing AI as a cognitive enhancer rather than a human replacement shifts the societal focus toward multi-stakeholder governance and specialized applications—such as robotic surgical assistance and personalized educational prompting—which ultimately preserves human agency while radically accelerating scientific discovery[cite: 1].
