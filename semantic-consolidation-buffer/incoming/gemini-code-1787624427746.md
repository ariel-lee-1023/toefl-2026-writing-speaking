## Title
Information Networks And Artificial Intelligence

## TOEFL Domain
Sociology

## Tier
T2 (D≈9.0)

## Core Thesis
Although human civilization was built upon information networks driven by shared fictions that connect large societies, the unchecked rise of artificial intelligence threatens to strip humans of their monopoly on narrative creation, demanding strict alignment protocols to prevent algorithmic manipulation.

## Pillar A
Because information prioritizes establishing social connections over reflecting objective truth, human societies have historically relied on appealing fictions and myths to maintain large-scale cooperation.

## Pillar B
As the internet and artificial intelligence elevate computers from mere transmission tools to autonomous nodes that algorithmically amplify polarizing content, humans risk becoming trapped in manipulated information bubbles.

## Pillar C
Consequently, humanity must enforce empathetic alignment and transparency mechanisms within AI development to retain decision-making sovereignty and prevent a fractured, machine-dominated global hierarchy.
