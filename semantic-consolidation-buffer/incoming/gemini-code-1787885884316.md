## Title
Educational Salon AI-Driven Constructivist Learning Models

## TOEFL Domain
Education

## Tier
T4 (D≈21)

## Core Thesis
Although artificial intelligence lowers technical execution barriers for young learners, meaningful educational transformation requires shifting pedagogy from passive content consumption to active, problem-driven constructivist creation.

## Pillar A
Passive rote learning and labeled test drills store static knowledge in memory without developing a student's capacity to deploy concepts in complex, real-world scenarios.

## Pillar B
Leveraging AI as a co-creative partner allows students to identify genuine problems, iterate on interactive software prototypes, and transition static information into operational problem-solving skills.

## Pillar C
Structuring learning around authentic exploration and multi-agent collaboration cultivates human-centric critical judgment, ensuring technological tools elevate rather than replace human creative agency.
