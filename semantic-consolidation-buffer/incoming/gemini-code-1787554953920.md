## Title
Reclaiming Liberal Education

## TOEFL Domain
Education

## Tier
T3 (D≈16)

## Core Thesis
Although a liberal education was originally designed to cultivate the civic responsibilities and intellectual humility required for a functioning free society, American universities have increasingly abandoned this mission in favor of ideological indoctrination, necessitating a multi-front reform effort to restore viewpoint diversity and rigorous academic inquiry.

## Pillar A
Over the past several decades, the academic establishment has been gradually overtaken by a blend of postmodern relativism and radical political activism -> resulting in a self-replicating faculty culture that suppresses dissenting viewpoints -> and prioritizes the transmission of specific political orthodoxies over the objective exploration of historical truths.

## Pillar B
This ideological capture has manifested in the implementation of rigid speech codes, the penalization of empirical analysis that challenges preferred narratives, and the reliance on peer-review systems that act as ideological gatekeepers -> which collectively destroy the foundational Socratic dialogue championed by classical liberal thinkers.

## Pillar C
To counter this deep-seated institutional decay, reform cannot rely solely on the actions of individual university presidents -> but must instead involve a comprehensive strategy that includes establishing new civic thought programs and creating alternative educational institutions -> aiming to systematically rebuild a robust academic environment.
