## Title
The Truth About Cancer

## TOEFL Domain
Biology

## Tier
T2 (D≈10.0)

## Core Thesis
Although cancer is widely feared as an insurmountable and homogenous disease, it is actually a highly complex group of endogenous cellular mutations that can be systematically managed through rigorous preventative screening, lifestyle modifications, and advanced targeted therapies.

## Pillar A
Because malignant tumors originate from the body's own mutating cells and constantly develop drug resistance, traditional broad-spectrum treatments like chemotherapy often result in severe collateral damage to healthy tissues.

## Pillar B
By identifying specific genetic mutations and leveraging the body's natural defense mechanisms, modern medicine has developed targeted therapies and immunotherapies to attack cancer cells with high precision.

## Pillar C
Consequently, integrating these advanced combinatorial treatments with widespread early screening and the active avoidance of known carcinogens significantly improves patient survival rates and overall prognosis.
