## Title
Strategic Adaptation in AI Era

## TOEFL Domain
Business Strategy

## Tier
T2 (D≈7)

## Core Thesis
While traditional corporate planning relies on top-down, predictive roadmaps modeled after central GPS systems, the rapid iteration cycles of the AI era require organizations to adopt decentralized, observation-based adaptation methods akin to autonomous satellite navigation.

## Pillar A
The historical reliance on long-term, predictive business models is fundamentally failing because the underlying technological capabilities and market conditions are now changing every few months, rendering top-down strategic forecasts obsolete almost immediately.

## Pillar B
Instead of waiting for perfect master plans, individuals and teams must deploy small-scale, imperfect experiments in their daily workflows, using the discrepancy between expected and actual results as real-time coordinate data to calibrate their next moves.

## Pillar C
When these localized, trial-and-error observations are systematically shared across the organization, the collective network continuously corrects its shared operational catalog, transforming isolated individual efficiencies into systemic organizational agility.
