## Title
Poor Charlies Almanack And Mental Models

## TOEFL Domain
Business Management

## Tier
T2 (D≈7.3)

## Core Thesis
Although many investors rely on superficial financial metrics and monolithic analytical frameworks, achieving sustained financial success requires synthesizing a multidisciplinary mental model and strictly adhering to value investing within one's established circle of competence.

## Pillar A
Because the universe operates as a highly complex and interconnected system, relying on isolated, single-discipline thinking inevitably leads to distorted perceptions and catastrophic strategic misjudgments.

## Pillar B
By applying reverse engineering to avoid flawed knowledge structures and deliberately combining insights from diverse fields like psychology, economics, and mathematics, an individual can construct a robust cognitive framework.

## Pillar C
This comprehensive mental flexibility enables investors to accurately assess intrinsic corporate value, identify sustainable competitive moats, and execute highly rational, long-term capital allocations.
