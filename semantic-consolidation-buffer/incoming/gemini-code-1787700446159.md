## Title
Strategic Boundary Redefinition for Growth

## TOEFL Domain
Business Administration

## Tier
T3 (D≈18)

## Core Thesis
Although commercial enterprises frequently encounter growth plateaus when defining their operations through rigid product categories, sustainable expansion requires redefining business boundaries around fundamental consumer needs and identifying market discontinuities.

## Pillar A
Pillar A (Context/Problem): Defining corporate strategy around specific physical goods or technologies creates artificial growth boundaries -> intense intra-category competition saturates existing consumer markets -> organizations experience growth stagnation or resort to unsustainable, margin-eroding expansion tactics.

## Pillar B
Pillar B (Mechanism/Intervention): Transitioning from a single-track product model to a multi-track perspective re-anchors corporate strategy on underlying human demands -> systematic analysis identifies market discontinuities and unserved consumer niches left by established competitors -> firms deploy targeted offerings that address holistic customer problems.

## Pillar C
Pillar C (Implication/Result): Aligning internal organizational culture with core consumer-centric goals unlocks expanded market opportunities -> value capture shifts from static product share to sustainable customer wallet share -> enterprises secure long-term, resilient growth across evolving industry paradigms.
