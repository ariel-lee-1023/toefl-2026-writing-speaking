## Title
Executive Management And Effectiveness

## TOEFL Domain
Business Management

## Tier
T2 (D≈5.8)

## Core Thesis
Although many consider constant busyness to be a sign of hard work, true management effectiveness requires an executive to focus on contributions, deliberate time management, leveraging strengths, and making fewer but principled decisions.

## Pillar A
Because the shift to knowledge work makes productivity difficult to measure, executives easily become prisoners of organizational routines and suffer from severe time fragmentation[cite: 14].

## Pillar B
By deliberately recording time usage, eliminating wasteful activities, focusing on people's strengths rather than weaknesses, and setting up systematic rules for recurring issues, executives regain control over their focus[cite: 14].

## Pillar C
This deliberate self-management translates individual knowledge into tangible organizational contributions and ensures that the organization runs smoothly and uneventfully rather than operating in a constant state of crisis[cite: 14].
